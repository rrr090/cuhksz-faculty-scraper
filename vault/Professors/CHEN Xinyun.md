## Biography

Professor Chen received her B.A. in Mathematics from Peking University in 2009 and her Ph.D. in Operations Research from Columbia University in 2014. Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, she was an assistant professor at Stony Brook University and Wuhan University. Her research interests include queueing theory, stochastic simulation, and reinforcement learning and their applications to health care and communication networks. She has published papers in Mathematics of Operations Research, Operations Research, Management Science, and at ICLR. Her research has been supported by multiple grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and Huawei Technologies. She currently serves on the editorial boards of Operations Research, INFORMS Journal on Computing, and Stochastic Systems.
