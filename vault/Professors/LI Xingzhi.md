## Biography

Dr. Li Graduated from ‌Baotou Medical College‌ with a degree in Clinical Medicine in 1995. Received a master's degree from ‌Qingdao University‌ in 2006. Earned a doctoral degree in Surgery from Sun Yat-sen‌ University in 2009. He worked in the key specialty of Urology at ‌Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region People's Hospital‌ since 2009, serving as the deputy director of the department for 5 years. Dr. Li was Introduced as a ‌"Shenlong Talent"‌ to lead the Urology Department at ‌Longgang District People's Hospital‌ in 2019, serving as the department head and academic leader. His research focus are specialized in basic and clinical research in Urology, as well as the application and promotion of minimally invasive techniques. Currently, he holds multiple positions including department head, doctoral supervisor, postdoctoral supervisor, and member of various professional committees.‌
