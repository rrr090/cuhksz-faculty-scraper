## Biography

Lun Yu is the Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences from Northwestern University in 2020. In 2021, he won the Nemhausser’s Best Student Dissertation Prize. Lun’s research interests include service operations, queueing, supply chain management, optimal control, and machine learning.
