## Biography

Bing-Yi JING is a Duan Family Presidential Chair Professor and the Associate Dean (School Development & Student Affairs) at the School of Artificial intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen. He is a recipient of the Second Prize of the National Natural Science Award, and a recipient of the Second Prize of the Ministry of Education’s Natural Science Award for Universities. He is a Fellow of the American Statistical Association (ASA Fellow), a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics (IMS Fellow), and an Elected Member of the International Statistical Institute (ISI Elected Member). He serves as the Chairman of the Multivariate Analysis Committee of the Chinese Society for Applied Statistics. He has successively held the position of associate editor for several international academic journals. He has published over 140 papers in top journals and conferences in the fields of probability and statistics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence—including AoS, JRSS-B, JASA, Biometrika, AoP, JoE, JMLR, NeurIPS, ICLR, etc. With extensive experience in industry collaborations, he has been honored with the Huawei Spark Award and the Huawei Excellent Collaborative Achievement Award.
