## Biography

Prof. Gao Huizhi received her BSc from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2017, her MPH from The University of Hong Kong in 2018, and her PhD (Supervisor: Prof. Benjamin J. Cowling) from The University of Hong Kong in 2023. She then served as a State‑funded Postdoctoral Fellow at Peking University from 2024 to 2026. She joined the School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in May 2026. As an epidemiologist, Prof. Gao’s research focuses on infectious disease epidemiology, population health and aging, social behaviors, and health inequality. Her work has contributed insights into waning vaccine immunity, pandemic fatigue, and the effectiveness of nonpharmaceutical interventions. She also investigates the social determinants of mental health among middle‑aged and older adults, as well as the spatiotemporal evolution of healthcare resource allocation in China. Her current research areas include: (1) spatial and social inequalities in health service access and utilization; (2) infectious disease dynamics and behavioral drivers of transmission, with an emphasis on vaccine‑preventable diseases and emerging respiratory viruses; and (3) the impact of internet use and social relationships on mental health in aging populations.
