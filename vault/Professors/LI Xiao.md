## Biography

Professor Xiao Li is an Associate Professor at the School of Data Science (SDS), The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He earned his Ph.D. degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2020. Professor Li’s research focuses on stochastic, nonsmooth, nonconvex optimization, and applying optimization techniques to improve large language model training in a practical sense.
