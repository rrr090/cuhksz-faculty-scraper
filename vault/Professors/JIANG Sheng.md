## Biography

Sheng Jiang is currently an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) in the School of Data Science at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. His research interests primarily focus on developing Bayesian nonparametric methods to analyze complex datasets in social and scientific applications, striking a balance between modeling flexibility and scientific interpretability. Specifically, he works on Gaussian process methods, traffic flow network data analysis, Bayesian Cryo-EM data analysis, and variational Bayes. His previous work on Bayesian nonparametric methods with Gaussian process priors has been published in leading statistics journals such as AOS and JASA. Before moving to Shenzhen, he served as a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of California, Santa Cruz, for 2.5 years. He also spent one semester as a postdoctoral associate at Duke University, co-advised by Surya Tokdar and Alexander Volfovsky. He completed his Ph.D. in Statistics at Duke University under the supervision of Surya Tokdar and holds a B.A. in Economics from Tsinghua University.
