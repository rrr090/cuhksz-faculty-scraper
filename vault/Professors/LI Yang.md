## Biography

LI Yang is an Associate Professor at School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University and her bachelor’s degree from Smith College. Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, she served as faculty at Tsinghua-Berkeley Shenzhen Institute and Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School. Her research focuses on reliable and interpretable machine learning for trustworthy AI, with particular interests in transferability quantification, continual learning, and parsimonious model design, with applications to multimodal medical image understanding. She is a recipient of the Pengcheng Peacock Talent (Level C) and has received multiple grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China. She is a Technical Committee member of the IVMSP Committee of the IEEE Signal Processing Society, and serves as an Associate Editor for Franklin Open and Publicity Editor for IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Signal Processing.
