## Biography

Dr. Aditi Dudeja is a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before this, she was a postdoctoral fellow in the Big Data Algorithms group at the University of Salzburg. She studies graph problems in streaming, dynamic, and online settings.
