## Biography

Prof. Jenkin Tsui completed his medical training at the University of St Andrews (BSc Hons in Medicine) and the University of Edinburgh (MBChB), followed by an Internal Medicine residency and Fellowship at the University of Calgary. He is board-certified by both the American Board of Internal Medicine and the Royal College of Physicians of Canada. Dedicated to advancing medical education, Prof. Tsui integrates point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) and robotics into training, championing early bedside exposure and sonoanatomy teaching to strengthen foundational skills. To enhance clinical medicine, he is designing a tailored curriculum for the trilingual (English, Mandarin, Cantonese) context of the Greater Bay Area to better serve its diverse patient population. Beyond curriculum design, Prof. Tsui bridges international and domestic medical students. Having trained across the United Kingdom, United States, and Canadian healthcare education systems, he believes world-class education demands a collaborative, inclusive environment. At CUHK-Shenzhen, he actively unites international and Chinese learners through peer learning, cross-cultural communication, and mutual respect from the first clinical encounters. His broader vision: a new generation of physicians who are globally minded, linguistically agile, and ready to collaborate across borders. Prof. Tsui firmly believes that to train the doctors of the future, we must equip today's learners with tomorrow's tools—because they will set the future standard of care.
