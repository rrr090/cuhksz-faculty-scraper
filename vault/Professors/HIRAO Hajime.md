## Biography

Hajime Hirao received his BEng and MEng degrees from Kyoto University in 1998 and 2000, respectively, and his PhD from the University of Tokyo in 2004. He completed postdoctoral studies at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Emory University, and Kyoto University. He has been engaged in computational chemistry research at various institutions such as Novartis, Nanyang Technological University, and City University of Hong Kong. His research interests include the computational exploration and prediction of the properties of drug-metabolizing cytochrome P450 enzymes and their health implications.
