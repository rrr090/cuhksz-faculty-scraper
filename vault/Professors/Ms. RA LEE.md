## Biography

RA LEE, born in South Korea, is currently a lecturer at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her musical training at the Yong Siew Toh Conservatory of Music in Singapore, the Hochschule für Musik, Theater und Medien Hannover, and the Mozarteum University Salzburg. After completing the prestigious Konzertexamen in Germany, she established herself as a versatile musician active as a soloist, chamber musician, and orchestral performer. Her orchestral career includes positions with the Bruckner Orchestra Linz and the Göttingen Symphony Orchestra, where she served as 2nd solo cellist. RA LEE has appeared as a soloist, chamber musician, and orchestral musician in major cultural centers throughout Europe, Asia, North America, and South America, including the United States. She has performed at prominent concert venues across Europe and Asia, including the Vienna Musikverein, Linz Brucknerhaus, Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, and the Leipzig Mendelssohn-Haus in Germany. In Korea, she has appeared at leading venues such as the Seoul Arts Center, the Lotte Concert Hall, and the Seongnam Arts Center. She is a laureate of several international competitions, including the Gisborne International Music Competition in New Zealand and the Schoenfeld International String Competition, among others.
