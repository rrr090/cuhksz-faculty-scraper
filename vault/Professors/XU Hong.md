## Biography

XU Hong is a renowned pianist and currently serves as an Associate Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He is a Yangtze River Piano Artist, an expert at the Degree Authentication Center of the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China, a council member of the Piano Society of the China Musicians Association, and a visiting professor at the Wuhan Conservatory of Music. He studied at the Eastman School of Music and The Juilliard School in the United States, where his teachers included Jiang Liping, Douglas Humpherys, Lowenthal, and McDonald. In 2010, he was awarded Juilliard's annual Artist Diploma in Piano—the only one given in the piano major that year—and subsequently returned to China to teach. The New York Times has described him as "a Chinese pianist with brilliant technique, keen insight, and unlimited potential," while The Times called him "an artist to watch." A winner of international competitions including Honens, Bachauer, and Hilton Head, XU Hong has performed with many world-class masters, among them Ashkenazy, Pletnev, Seguin, Sir Mark Elder, Hans Graf, and Markus Stenz. He has toured at home and abroad at the invitation of the London Philharmonic Orchestra, the Hallé Orchestra, and the China NCPA Orchestra, and has collaborated on piano concertos with ensembles including the BBC Symphony Orchestra, the Russian National Orchestra, the Portuguese National Symphony Orchestra, the Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra, the Calgary Philharmonic Orchestra, the Macedonian National Philharmonic Orchestra, and, in China, the Beijing Symphony Orchestra, the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra, and the Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra. His performances have taken him to major halls around the world, including the Royal Festival Hall, the Barbican Centre, Wigmore Hall, the Kennedy Center, Carnegie Hall, Place des Arts, and the Seoul Arts Center, as well as venues in China, including the National Centre for the Performing Arts, the Great Hall of the People, the Hong Kong Cultural Centre, and the National Theater & Concert Hall in Taipei. From 2010 to 2023, XU Hong taught in the Piano Department of the Wuhan Conservatory of Music, where his students achieved outstanding results and won major international and domestic competition awards. His online masterclass earned widespread acclaim and became one of the longest-running public courses serialized on the internet. As a speaker, he has given numerous lectures and masterclasses at home and abroad, including at the Eastman School of Music, Oberlin Conservatory of Music, San Francisco Conservatory of Music, and New York University in the United States; Trinity College of Music in London; the University of Auckland in New Zealand; the University of Melbourne in Australia; Seoul National University in Korea; and, in China, the Central Conservatory of Music, National Taiwan University of the Arts, the Xi'an Conservatory of Music, the Xinghai Conservatory of Music, and the Zhejiang Conservatory of Music. In summer, he teaches at the Amalfi International Piano Festival in Italy, the Leon Fleisher Music Festival in Washington; in winter, he teaches at the New Zealand International Piano Festival. He has served on juries for many major domestic and international competitions, including the Gina Bachauer International Piano Competition in the United States, the Eastman International Piano Competition, the Fou Ts'ong International Concerto Competition, and the Kawai Asian Piano Competition. In January 2027, he will be the only Chinese pianist on the jury of the renowned Bösendorfer International Piano Competition in Phoenix, United States. XU Hong is also a tireless advocate of new music and has given world premieres of works by composers such as Frederic Rzewski and Gao Ping. In chamber music, he has frequently collaborated with renowned musicians such as Lü Siqing, Qin Liwei, Zhang Liping, Zhou Zhengzhong, and Wang Tao. In recent years, XU Hong has devoted himself to the study of Mozart's music and style, giving numerous popular and academic lectures in many places. He will record a complete set of Mozart sonatas for the NCPA Classics and present further Mozart-themed recitals in concert.
