## Biography

Lecturer Wang Hongbin obtained his Bachelor of Medicine degree from Soochow University, in 2019 and received his Master of Medicine degree from Soochow University in 2021. He then finished standardized residency training in Jiangnan University Medical Center from 2021 to 2023. Lecturer Wang Hongbin joined the Second Affiliated Hospital of the Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen) as a dermatologist in September of 2023. Currently, he serves as a committee member of the Dermatology Professional Committee of Shenzhen Health Management Association and the Medical Cosmetology Professional Committee of Shenzhen Association of Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine. His research interests focus on the targeted therapy for psoriasis.
