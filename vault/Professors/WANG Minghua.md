## Biography

Adjunct Assistant Professor Wang graduated from the Chongqing Medical University in 1995 and obtained her doctorate from the Chongqing Medical University in 2008. She joined The Second Affiliated Hospital, School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2020. Her research area related to endometriosis and related tumor; EBV and lymphoma; glioma, and has published widely on these areas. Adjunct Assistant Professor Wang was a member of breast diseases group of the 11th,12th and 13th Committee of pathology branch, Chinese Medical Association since 2014, Vice chairman of Hainan Pathology Society, Chinese Medical Association (2019-2022), member of the 1st and 2sec molecular diagnosis and treatment Committee (academic group) of glioma Professional Committee of Chinese Medical Doctor Association (2017-2022), vice president of Shenzhen Pathologist Association (2023-2026), and member of the standing committee of pathology branch, Shenzhen Medical Association (2024-2028).
