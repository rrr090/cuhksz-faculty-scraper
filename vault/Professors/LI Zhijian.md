## Biography

Prof. Zhijian Li is an Assistant Professor and Principal Investigator at the School of Medicine and the Warshel Institute for Computational Biology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. from RWTH Aachen University, Germany, in 2022. Following his doctoral studies, he conducted postdoctoral research at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard in the United States before joining CUHK-Shenzhen in 2026. Prof. Li’s research lies at the intersection of computational biology, genomics, and artificial intelligence. His work focuses on developing innovative computational methods and machine learning models to integrate and analyze single-cell, spatial, and multimodal omics data, with the goal of understanding gene regulatory mechanisms underlying cell identity, cell state transitions, and disease development. His research interests include single-cell and spatial multiomics integration, inference of transcription factor activity and cis-regulatory interactions, deep learning for regulatory sequence modeling, and computational approaches to uncovering disease mechanisms and identifying therapeutic targets. Prof. Li has published more than 20 peer-reviewed research articles in leading scientific journals, including Nature, Nature Communications, Genome Biology, and Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases , with multiple publications as first or co-first author. His research has received over 2,000 citations. At CUHK-Shenzhen, Prof. Li leads the Computation Omics Laboratory, which develops interpretable and scalable computational methods by integrating statistical modeling, machine learning, and cutting-edge omics technologies. His laboratory aims to uncover fundamental principles of gene regulation and advance the application of artificial intelligence to disease biology and precision medicine.
