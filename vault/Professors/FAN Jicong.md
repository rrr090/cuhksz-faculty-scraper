## Biography

Jicong Fan is currently an Assistant Professor, Presidential Young Scholar, and PhD supervisor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Professor Fan received his PhD from the Department of Electronic Engineering at City University of Hong Kong. Before joining The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Cornell University. He also previously held research positions at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and the University of Hong Kong. Professor Fan's research interests are artificial intelligence and machine learning. He has conducted extensive research in matrix/tensor methods, clustering algorithms, anomaly detection, graph learning, automated machine learning, and other areas. He has published more than 70 papers in renowned academic journals and prestigious international conferences, such as IEEE TSP/TIP/TNNLS/TII, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, KDD, CVPR, and AAAI. He is a Senior Member of IEEE. He currently serves as Associate Editor/Action Editor for the journals Pattern Recognition, Neural Processing Letters, and TMLR, as Area Chair for the international AI conferences NeurIPS, ICLR, and ICML, and as SPC for conferences such as AAAI. He has served as Principal Investigator for projects including the National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Scientists Fund, the NSFC General Program, and the Guangdong Provincial General Program. He received the First Prize of the Natural Science Award of the Chinese Association of Automation in 2023. He has been continuously listed in Stanford University/Elsevier's "World's Top 2% Scientists" for 2023–2025 and was included in the 2025 Career-Long Scientific Impact list. Professor Fan is looking for PhD student, Postdoc, research assistant, and visiting student. If you are interested in these positions, please contact via fanjicong@cuhk.edu.cn.
