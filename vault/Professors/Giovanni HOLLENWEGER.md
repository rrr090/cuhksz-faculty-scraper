## Biography

Prof. Giovanni Hollenweger is an Assistant Professor in the Division of Global and Area Studies. His research focuses on conflict resolution in civil wars, investigating how civilian inclusion in rebel governance affects rebel-state bargaining and rebel-to-party transformation. He also conducts research in political mobilization, contentious politics and religious-secular political cleavages. His research has been published in the British Journal of Political Science.
