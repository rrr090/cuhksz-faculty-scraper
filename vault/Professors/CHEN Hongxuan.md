## Biography

Dr. Hongxuan Chen is an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at the School of Data Science at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. and B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. As a computer science education researcher, his work bridges theory and classroom practice. His research focuses on investigating cognitive bottlenecks in complex computing topics, developing tailored pedagogical interventions, and exploring how collaborative learning dynamics and curriculum design impact student success. Ultimately, his work aims to foster inclusive, evidence-based learning environments that make computer science more accessible.
