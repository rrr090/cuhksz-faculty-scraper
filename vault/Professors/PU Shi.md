## Biography

Dr. Shi Pu is an a ssociate professor and presidential young fellow at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, he conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Florida, Arizona State University, and Boston University. He received a B.S. degree from Peking University in 2012, and a Ph.D. degree in Systems Engineering from the University of Virginia in 2016. His main research interests lie in distributed optimization and large-scale machine learning. He has published over 20 papers as first or corresponding author in leading journals such as Operations Research, Mathematical Programming, IEEE TAC and IEEE TSP. One of his papers has been selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper, and his work has been cited more than 1,000 times.
