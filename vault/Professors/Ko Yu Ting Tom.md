## Biography

Tom KO currently serves as a full-time Associate Professor at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI) and Deputy Director of the LIMA Center. Previously, he worked as a Research Scientist at ByteDance AI Lab, an Assistant Professor at the Southern University of Science and Technology, and a Researcher at Huawei’s Noah’s Ark Lab. He received his PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2014. He has published over 50 research papers in speech processing and natural language processing, with more than 6,000 citations. Two of his pioneering papers on speech augmentation have each received over 1,000 citations.
