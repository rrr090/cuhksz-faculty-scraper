## Biography

Dr. Sidong Feng is an Assistant Professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Software Engineering from Monash University in 2026, with the Dean's Award for Research Excellence. He obtained his Bachelor’s degree with Honours from The Australian National University in 2019. His research lies at the intersection of Software Engineering (SE) and Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), with a multidisciplinary focus on intelligent software design, development, testing, and maintenance. His work has resulted in influential publications at top-tier SE conferences, including ICSE, FSE, ASE, and ISSTA, as well as leading HCI venues such as TOCHI, CHI, UIST, CSCW, and IUI. His contributions have been recognized with the ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Award at ICSE 2023 and the Highlight Paper at IUI 2021. He has strong collaborations with industry partners including Tencent, ByteDance, Alibaba, and Google, and his research has been widely adopted in real-world software development practices within these companies. He is also a recipient of the 2025 Amazon Research Award. He serves as the organizer of the Workshop on User Interface and Experience for Software Engineering (UISE) and as a program committee member for several top-tier conferences and journals. He is also recognized as a Distinguished Reviewer at ASE 2025.
