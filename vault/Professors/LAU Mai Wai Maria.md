## Biography

Dr. LAU received her bachelor degree from the University of Wisconsin with double majors in (1) Medical Microbiology and Immunology and (2) Bacteriology. After graduation, she worked in a Clinical Microbiology Laboratory in New York while obtaining her degree in Master of Public Health at the State University of New York. She received her doctorate degree in Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine at the New York Institute of Technology. Dr. LAU completed her Family Medicine residency training in New York and became Board Certified Family Medicine Physician in the USA. She practiced medicine in the area of Family Medicine for about a decade in California, USA before joining The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.
