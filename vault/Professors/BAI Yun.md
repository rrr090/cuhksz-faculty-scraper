## Biography

Prof. Yun Bai works at School of Medicine, CUHK-Shenzhen as an Associate Professor (Teaching). Prof. Yun Bai got her Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy from Peking University Health Science Center, Master's degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Tsinghua University, and Ph.D. degree in Pharmaceutical Sciences from University of Southern California. Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, Prof. Bai worked as an Associate Professor at the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences of Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine. Prof. Bai has 24 publications so far, including some high impact scientific journals such as PNAS and Pharmaceutical Research . She has one US patent. Her research areas include protein/peptide drug oral and transdermal delivery, recombinant fusion protein development, mechanism study of drug adverse effect using traditional pharmacology method and pharmacogenomic tools.
