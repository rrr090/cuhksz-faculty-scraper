## Biography

Lecturer ZHU graduated from Guangzhou Medical University in 2015 and received her master's degree from Zunyi Medical College in 2018. After graduation, she worked at Longgang District People's Hospital of Shenzhen. Her research area related to mechanisms of general anesthesia and general anesthetics. Lecturer ZHU is currently the technical backbone of Shenzhen Longgang District People's Hospital Class II full-time talent. In August 2022, she became an attending physician in the Department of Anesthesiology at 2nd affiliated hospital of CUHKSZ. In April 2019, she participated in the project which is mostcare to monitor the accuracy of SVV and PPV in predicting volume response of patients undergoing laparoscopic surgery at different surgical positions.
