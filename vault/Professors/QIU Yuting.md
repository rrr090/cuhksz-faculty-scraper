## Biography

Lecturer Qiu Yuting graduated from Tianjin Medical University in 2023, obtained her master's degree from the same institution in 2023, and has been working at the Second Affiliated Hospital of The CUHK-SZ since July 2023. Her research areas include chronic disease management, primary health care, health promotion and preventive medicine, doctor-patient relationship and communication, multimorbidity and geriatric medicine, mental health and general practice services, and general practice education and training. Lecturer Qiu Yuting currently serves as a clinical skills instructor at the Second Affiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong(Shenzhen)and has won awards in multiple teaching competitions.
