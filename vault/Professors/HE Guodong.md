## Biography

Professor Guodong He has long focused on the molecular mechanisms of G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) signaling and GPCR-targeted drug discovery. He received his Ph.D. from Tsinghua University in 2024 and completed his postdoctoral training at the School of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Tsinghua University, where he also served as an Assistant Researcher. In August 2026, Professor He joined the School of Medicine and the Kobilka Institute of Innovative Drug Discovery at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where he established the Laboratory of Receptor Pharmacology and Translational Therapeutics and currently serves as an Assistant Professor.
