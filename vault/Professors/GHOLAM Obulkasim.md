## Biography

Dr. Obulkasim graduated from the Xinjiang Medical University and obtained his academic master’s degree in 2019. Then he completed his surgical residency training from August 2019 to August 2022 at The University of Hong Kong-Shenzhen Hospital. During the time of residency training, he was awarded as excellent trainee of HKU-SZ Hospital. Moreover, he was successfully acepted for MSc Trauma Sciences program (PT) at QMUL, while one of his research outcomes was accepted for International Surgical Week (ISW) in 2022 as poster presentation. In July 2020, he was registered as Basic Surgical Trainee of Hong Kong Intercollegiate Board of Surgical Colleges . Dr. Obulkasim joined The Second Affiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen as a general surgery resident in 2022, focusing on surgical treatment for GI and HBP diseases. He became an attending surgeon in April 2024. Dr. Obulkasim is also a member of multiple professional committees of Shenzhen Medical Doctor Association, which include the member of Shenzhen Medical Doctor Association Professional Committee of Multidisciplinary Oncology Diagnosis and Treatment, Committee of Hernia and Abdominal Wall Surgeons, Committee of Trauma. As a clinician-scientist-educator driven by a triad of passions: pioneering innovative teaching methods, advancing medical English proficiency, and meticulously transferring surgical skills to the next generation. His philosophy centers on educating competent, confident, and globally communicative surgeons. At the core of his practice is the integration of advanced clinical expertise in minimally invasive surgery with a dedicated academic role, where he supervises and train residents. He actively promotes the usage of English language teaching to both students and to medical staff, ensuring that technical excellence is supported by the language skills necessary for international academic contribution and multidisciplinary collaboration.
