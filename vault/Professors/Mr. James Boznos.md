## Biography

A versatile musician, James Boznos (b. 1969, Chicago) has served as the Principal Timpanist of the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra since 2000. Boznos has appeared as a soloist with the HK Phil on three occasions, performing both of William Kraft’s timpani concertos as well as his own Concerto for Timpani , Nr. 1. In 2019, the China Philharmonic and Guangzhou Symphony co-commissioned and premiered his Concerto Nr. 2 “Pavilions” for extended timpani, orchestra, and electronics, featuring the composer as soloist. He has taught at Hong Kong University and The Academy for the Performing Arts, and has performed frequently as guest principal with major orchestras across Asia, including those in Beijing, Guangzhou, Seoul, Singapore, Kanazawa, and Taichung. Prior to his appointment with the HK Phil, Boznos performed regularly with the Chicago Symphony Orchestra and leading contemporary music ensembles throughout Boston and Chicago. Notable early career highlights include performing Iannis Xenakis’ Psappha for solo percussion in the ancient amphitheatre at Delphi, Greece, and performing Dérive 1 and 2 at the Berlin Staatsoper for Pierre Boulez’s 75th birthday celebration, conducted by Boulez himself. As founder and director of the contemporary ensemble KLANG, he conducted over sixty modern works, including the US and Asian premieres of Stockhausen’s Donnerstags-Gruss . His conducting appearances also include the Taiwan Symphony Orchestra (NTSO), the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra, and the Chicago Chamber Orchestra. As a composer, Boznos has created an expansive catalogue of solo, chamber, and orchestral works that integrate instrumental, vocal, and electronic mediums. Recent premieres include 22.4 with Matthew Lau, as well as Cantata Nr. 2 “Nevu’ah”, Cantata Nr. 3 “Gabriel”, and Cantata Nr. 4 “Unseen Things” with the Hong Kong Bach Choir. The Hong Kong Philharmonic has performed an extensive selection of his works, including Oikogeneia , Social Distancing , Go , Table-Music I , Concerto for Piccolo , Dragon Factors under Jaap van Zweden, and Cantata Nr. 5 “The Valley of the Bones”. His compositions are published internationally by Edition Svitzer .
