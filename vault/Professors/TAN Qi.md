## Biography

Lecturer TAN graduated from Tongji Medical College of Huazhong University of Science & Technology and obtained her bachelor degree in 2005. As an undergraduate, her first foreign language was German and went to Germany for a two-month internship in 2004. She graduated from Ji ’nan University and obtained her master degree in 2008. She joined Longgang district people’s hospital of Shenzhen as a pathologist in the same year. In 2010, she went to the Pathology Department of the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University for a year of advanced study. She obtained her doctorate from Southern Medical University in 2020. Her research area related to cervical cancer and colorectal cancer. She presided over one municipal-level research project and two district-level research projects. Qi Tan has published several articles on these areas.
