## Biography

DU Mengnan is an Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University. Before joining CUHK–Shenzhen, he served as an Assistant Professor at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). He has also held positions or internships at major tech companies, including Microsoft Research (MSR), Adobe Research, Intel, Baidu Research, and JD Explore Academy. His research primarily focuses on trustworthy large language models (LLMs), with a particular emphasis on LLM Interpretability, Reasoning, Reinforcement Learning, and other foundational algorithm designs, as well as their practical applications. He has published over 100 papers with more than 11,000 Google Scholar citations. His contributions have been recognized with the Cisco Research Award, the NJIT Faculty Research Excellence Award, and inclusion in the Top 2% Scientist Worldwide list for 2024 and 2025 by Stanford University. Meanwhile, he is deeply involved in the academic community, regularly serving as a Senior Area Chair for EMNLP, and as an Area Chair for prestigious conferences such as ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, and AISTATS. He also serves as an Associate Editor for TMLR and Applied AI Letters.
