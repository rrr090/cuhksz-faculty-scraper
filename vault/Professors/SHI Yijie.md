## Biography

SHI Yijie, a renowned tenor, is currently the President of the Shanghai Opera House and a Professor at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. With exceptional artistry, he is an active presence on today's international opera stage. In 2007, he consecutively won four gold medals in international vocal competitions. He is the first Chinese singer to grace the "Rossini Opera Festival"—the premier institution for interpreting Rossini's works—and has served as a principal singer in operas for five consecutive years. His performance credits include the world's most prestigious opera houses and festivals: Teatro alla Scala (Milan), Teatro La Fenice (Venice), Teatro di San Carlo (Naples), Maggio Musicale Fiorentino (Florence), Teatro Real (Madrid), Gran Teatre del Liceu (Barcelona), Deutsche Oper Berlin (Berlin), Théâtre des Champs-Élysées (Paris), Théâtre du Capitole (Toulouse), Opéra de Marseille (Marseille), San Francisco Opera (San Francisco), Bolshoi Theatre (Moscow), Israeli Opera (Tel Aviv), Edinburgh International Festival (Edinburgh), Hong Kong Arts Festival (Hong Kong), and the National Centre for the Performing Arts (Beijing). He excels in performing works by Rossini, Donizetti, Bellini, and Mozart, and is internationally recognized as one of the finest interpreters of the Italian Bel Canto repertoire. His masterful and refined control over vocal purity, nuanced tonal shading, and seamless melodic lines allows him to interpret diverse musical styles with distinctive coloration. The precise yet expressive fusion of vocal beauty and poetic nuance has earned him wide acclaim and admiration from audiences worldwide. To date, he has performed a total of 41 operas globally, with signature roles including Il barbiere di Siviglia (Count Almaviva), La Cenerentola (Prince Ramiro), L'italiana in Algeri (Lindoro), Lucia di Lammermoor (Edgardo), La fille du régiment (Tonio), L'elisir d'amore (Nemorino), Rigoletto (Il Duca di Mantova), La Bohème (Rodolfo), Das Land des Lächelns (Prinz Sou-Chong) and Dream of the Red Chamber (Jia Baoyu). His flawless rendition of the 19 High C's in La fille du régiment —a demanding highlight of the tenor repertoire—solidified his distinguished status in the opera world. Since 2010, he has maintained a long-standing collaboration with renowned composer Xiaogang YE, beginning with the world premiere of the opera Song of Farewell and extending to performances in over ten major original works, including 4 Poems of Lingnan , Road to the Republic , Twilight of the Himalayas , and Du Fu's Thatched Cottage . Deeply devoted to Chinese culture, he has in recent years been committed to promoting and popularizing Chinese art songs both at home and abroad. His interpretations of songs such as "Nostalgia," "Three Wishes of the Rose," "I Live at the Head of the Yangtze River," and "How Can I Forget Her?" have captivated countless vocal students and enthusiasts. Since 2021, SHI Yijie has collaborated with pianist Chen Sa on the two-volume Chinese Art Songs album series, which has garnered enthusiastic acclaim from a vast audience of music lovers. With years of devotion to vocal education, he planned and led the Shanghai Opera House's 2026 flagship initiative, the Chinese Young Artist Program for Opera, inviting Teatro di San Carlo, Teatro La Fenice, and Teatro dell'Opera di Roma as international partners to cultivate young opera talents.
