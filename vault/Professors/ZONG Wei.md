## Biography

Wei Zong received dual bachelor’s degrees from Heriot-Watt University and Southwestern University of Finance and Economics in 2016, a master’s degree in Mathematical Statistics from the University of Cambridge in 2017, and a PhD in Biostatistics from the University of Pittsburgh in 2023. Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, she served as a Translational Lead Biostatistician at Sanofi, where she led biomarker data analyses for multiple real-world clinical studies. Her research lies at the intersection of statistical methodology and interdisciplinary biomedical applications, with a broad goal of advancing health data science. In particular, she develops statistical methods for high-dimensional data to support informed decision-making from large-scale datasets and foster scientific collaborations.
