## Biography

CHAN Wing Wah, JP, is currently serving as Consultant to both the AVP (Academic) and Acting Dean of the School of Music at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). He is also Chairman of the Composers and Authors Society of Hong Kong (a music copyright organization), Chairman of the Hong Kong Association of Choral Societies, a council member of the Hong Kong Children's Choir and Hong Kong Chinese Orchestra (HKCO), and Music Director of the Hong Kong Oratorio Society (HKOS) and Allegro Singer. In addition, he serves as an advisor to numerous groups, including the China Musicians Association, Hong Kong Dance Company, New Tune Chinese Orchestra, Windpipe Chinese Music Ensemble, and the Harmonic Singers. Prof. CHAN was the first Resident Composer of the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra. He has composed 10 symphonies and more than 200 musical works, including chamber and orchestral music with Chinese, Western, Japanese and Korean instruments, and songs for adults and children. Seven of his ten symphonies have been released on CD by Hugo Productions, performed by orchestras in Hong Kong SAR and Russia. The music scores of Symphony No. 5, The Three Kingdoms , and Symphony No. 6, Reunification , were published by People's Music Publishing House. His Symphony No. 8 and Symphony No. 10 were released on CD by the National Centre for the Performing Arts, Beijing. His music has received First Prize in the International Double Reed Society Composition Contest, USA; the Yoshiro Irino Memorial Award from the Asian Composers League, and several local awards. His works have been performed in over 30 countries, and his biography is included in the New Grove Dictionary of Music & Musicians Online and Oxford Music Online. In 2000, he was appointed a Justice of the Peace by the Government of the Hong Kong SAR. Since 1995, Prof. CHAN has served as Music Director of the HKOS (established in 1956), leading the choir in regular concerts and concert tours. In 2017, he conducted the HKOS and a string orchestra in San Francisco, in performances of his Symphony No. 8, This Boundless Land , for organ, choir and orchestra at the Herbst Theatre in San Francisco and the Walt Disney Concert Hall in Los Angeles. In 2019, he brought the HKOS together with the Vancouver Oratorio Society to Toronto for the premiere of his Symphony No. 9, Universal Harmony . In July 2022, his Symphony No.10 Spring and Autumn for Chinese Orchestra received its premiere in two concerts presented by the HKCO. In 2009, he was invited to become a Council Member of the China Musicians Association. Prof. CHAN received his Bachelor of Arts in Music from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), where he studied composition with Professor David Gwilt. He was then awarded a Canadian Commonwealth Scholarship to pursue postgraduate studies at the University of Toronto under the supervision of Professor John Beckwith, where he earned a Master of Music in Composition and a Doctor of Music in Composition. He previously served as a member of the Executive Committee of the International Society for Contemporary Music, Vice-Chairman of the Asian Composers' League, Professor of Music, Associate Dean of Arts, Chairman of the Department of Music at CUHK, and Associate Dean of the School of Music at CUHK-Shenzhen. He also dedicated a part of his time to public service, serving as an appointed Regional Council Member, a Member of the Hong Kong Arts Development Council, and Chairman of the Jockey Club Music and Dance Fund, among others.
