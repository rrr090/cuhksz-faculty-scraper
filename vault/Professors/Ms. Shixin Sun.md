## Biography

Shixin Sun, Opera Coach (Repetitor), is currently a faculty member at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She worked as a repetiteur in the Young Ensemble of the Semperoper Dresden and collaborated with the Staatskapelle Dresden as well as with internationally renowned singers. She began studying piano at an early age, and from 2007, studied with Prof. Zhou Ting and Prof. Luo Xiao. From 2012 to 2015, she attended the Middle School Affiliated with the Shanghai Conservatory of Music, majoring in orchestral conducting under Prof. Zhao Xiao'ou and Prof. Zhang Guoyong. From 2016 to 2022, she completed her bachelor's and master's degrees in opera coaching (Musiktheaterkorrepetition) at the Carl Maria von Weber University of Music in Dresden, Germany, studying with Prof. Karl-Heinz Knobloch, Prof. Eckehard Stier, and Prof. Valtteri Rauhalammi. She also received further training in German Lied accompaniment under the guidance of the renowned baritone, Prof. Olaf Baer. In 2021, she served as a guest repetiteur at the Serkowitzer Volksoper in Dresden. In the same year, she worked as an orchestral pianist at the Gerhart-Hauptmann-Theater Goerlitz-Zittau, where she took part in the rehearsals and performances of Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical Evita . From the 2022/23 to the 2023/24 seasons, she was engaged as a repetiteur with the Young Ensemble of the Semperoper Dresden, where she contributed to the rehearsals and performances of numerous opera productions, including La sonnambula , Orfeo , La Boheme , Tosca , Die Entfuehrung aus dem Serail , Attila , La Traviata , Haensel und Gretel , Der Freischuetz , and Die Zauberfloete . As an orchestral pianist, she frequently collaborated with the Staatskapelle Dresden, performing in both opera and symphonic concerts. She has also collaborated with conductors such as Antonello Allemandi, Johannes Fritsche, Evelino Pido, Jordi Bernacer, Wolfgang Katschner, Antonello Manacorda, Christoph Gedschold, and Gaetano d'Espinosa, and with internationally acclaimed singers including Rolando Villazón, Nikola Hillebrand, Elbenita Kajtazi, Georg Zeppenfeld, Johanni van Oostrum, Günther Groissböck, Emily Pogorelc, and Maxim Mirono.
