## Biography

Lecturer Zhong Ling Graduated from Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine in 2010 with a seven-year clinical program in integration Traditional Chinese and Western medicine (Gynecology). She worked in the Obstetrics and Gynecology Department of Shajing People's Hospital in Bao'an District, Shenzhen from 2010 to 2012. After standardized training for resident physicians at Xiangya Second Hospital from 2012 to 2014, she joined the gynecology department of Longgang District People's Hospital in Shenzhen. In 2018, she went to Fang Yigong's premature ovarian failure workshop of Institute of Acupuncture and Moxibustion Hospital of China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences for further study.
