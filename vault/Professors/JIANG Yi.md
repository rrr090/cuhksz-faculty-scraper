## Biography

Dr. Yi Jiang graduated from the Shihezi University in 2004 and obtained his doctorate from the Xinjiang Medical University in 2011. He joined The second Affiliated Hospital, School of MEDICINE, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2023. His research area related to imaging diagnosis of various systemic tumor diseases, particularly skilled in the imaging diagnosis of hepato-biliary and pediatric diseases and magnetic resonance-guided high-intensity focused ultrasound treatments. Dr. Yi Jiang has published widely on these areas. In 2018, Dr. Yi Jiang was selected as a candidate for the thirteenth batch of academic and technical leaders by the Sichuan Provincial Health Commission. And in 2022, he was recognized as one of the first distinguished position talents in Shenzhen. Professor Yi Jiang currently serves as a committee member of the Minimally Invasive Group at the Radiologists Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association, a committee member of the Molecular Imaging Group at the Chinese Medical Association Radiology Branch, a committee member of the Magnetic Resonance Professional Group at the Sichuan Medical Association Radiology Committee, and a young committee member of the Tumor Imaging Professional Committee at the Chinese Anti-Cancer Association. Dr. Yi Jiang has led one project funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, participated in two more, led a regional science and technology support project, a key laboratory project on echinococcosis at the regional level, and three city-level natural science foundation projects. In the past five years, Dr. Yi Jiang has published over 20 papers, including 5 SCI-indexed papers and 13 core papers, and has contributed to 5 monographs.
