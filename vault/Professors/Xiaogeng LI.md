## Biography

Xiaogeng LI, Director of the International /HK/MO/TW Office and an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, is an experienced concert producer, lecturer, writer, and video creator. His research spans music and arts management, music sociology, and applied musicology. He authored Parallel Universe: Music Across the East and West Through the Ages and Understand Music in Two Minutes: Decoding 100 Essential Western Masterpieces . He has taken part in multiple provincial and municipal philosophy and social science research projects, acted as lead drafter for Towards an International Performing Arts Capital: Global Case Studies and Strategies for Shenzhen , and published widely in music periodicals including People's Music and Music Weekly . Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, Xiaogeng LI worked in the U.S. as Public Relations Manager at Hilberry Theatre and Marketing Director at Bonstelle Theatre. He later spent several years as Artistic Planning Manager at the National Centre for the Performing Arts (NCPA), where his portfolio covered season planning, tour planning, artist management, commissioning and programme annotation. In the course of his management career, LI has collaborated with many world-renowned musicians including Christoph Eschenbach, Myung-Whun Chung, Jukka-Pekka Saraste, David Robertson, Lü Jia, Xincao Li, Vladimir Ashkenazy, Lang Lang, Wang Jian, Yuja Wang, Magnus Lindberg, Qigang Chen and Xiaogang Ye. He has helped shape the artistic planning for hundreds of concerts. Signature projects include the Opening Ceremony of the Beijing Winter Olympic Games, the Gala for the Belt and Road Summit for International Cooperation, and the NCPA Orchestra's North American Tour. Beyond academic research and concert production, Xiaogeng LI is also an advocate for classical music. Through his writings and pre-concert talks, he has gained recognition among a broad community of classical music enthusiasts. Music Rewind , a 50-episode music-history documentary series written and produced by him, premiered on Bilibili in early 2023. To date, the series has attracted tens of thousands of subscribers and amassed more than one million views. Xiaogeng LI is an alumnus of Wayne State University (U.S.) and the Central Conservatory of Music, holding an MFA in Theatre Management (terminal degree). He is also a saxophonist and was awarded third prize at the 3rd National Woodwind Competition hosted by the Central Conservatory of Music.
