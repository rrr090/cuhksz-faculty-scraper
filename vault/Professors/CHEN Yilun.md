## Biography

Dr. Chen is currentlly an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his B.S. in Mathematics from Peking University and his Ph.D. in Operations Research from Cornell University. His research is in Operations Research, with interests at the intersection of economics, theoretical computer science, and statistics. Motivated by applications in online platforms, marketplaces, and service systems, he studies data-driven decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on allocating limited resources in dynamic environments. His work has appeared in Management Science, Operations Research, and conferences such as ACM EC. He won First Place in the 2019 INFORMS George Nicholson Student Paper Competition and received the 2024 INFORMS Junior Faculty Interest Group Best Paper Award.
