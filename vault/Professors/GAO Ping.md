## Biography

Gao Ping, a composer-pianist, born in Chengdu, Sichuan Province, China, is currently a Professor of Composition at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He studied in the USA in the 1990s. In demand as a composer, he has received commissions and performances from musicians worldwide. His music has been featured in important festivals in Aspen, Dresden, and Wellington, as well as in concert halls such as Carnegie Hall, Lincoln Center, Salle Cortot, St. Petersburg Philharmonic, and the NCPA in Beijing. Gao Ping's two albums, Story Teller: Gao Ping Chamber Music and Piano Quintet "Mei, Lan, Zhu, Ju" , released on the Naxos label, were critically acclaimed. His music also appears on NCPA Classics, Yamaha, MDG, Atoll, Vermes, and Rattle record labels. In 2025, Ping Gao-Works for Piano was released on MDG (Germany) and immediately received high praise from magazines such as "Klassiks Heute." His other albums, such as Outside the Window (piano music) and Pure Wind (Music for Chinese ensemble), were released by China Records Corp. and People's Music. Piano Concerto No.1 was published by Shanghai Music Publisher. Since 2020, the Xi Nan Normal University Press has published a series of Gao's collections of music, including piano albums Outside the Window (2020), Wanderings (2022), and the song cycle Melodies Abandoned (2020). In recent years, works such as Four Not-Alike , Concerto for Multifunction Pianist and Chamber Orchestra, and Wind Bamboo, for Xiao and Harp, have expressed cross-cultural tendencies and pointed to a new direction in Gao's approach, combining Western and Chinese instruments. In 2016, Gao Ping was commissioned by artist Mr. Chen Danqing, the head of the Mu Xin Museum, to compile the music manuscripts by the late artist Mu Xin and to create new compositions based on this material. He premiered these compositions in a concert at the Wuzhen Theatre, which received significant critical response from the press as well as the general audience. As a required work for the 2024 Xiamen International Piano Competition, Window at Dusk , for solo piano, was premiered by over 20 young pianists from all over the world. The work received high praise from the performers, jury members, and audiences. Gao previously taught at the Conservatory of Music, Capital Normal University in Beijing, and the University of Canterbury in New Zealand. He was appointed as the 2018 Composer-in-Residence of Orchestra Wellington (New Zealand), premiering the orchestral songs Wild Cherry Tree .
