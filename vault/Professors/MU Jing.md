## Biography

Prof. Jing Mu is currently an assistant professor at School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her Bachelor’s degree from Nankai University and his PhD degree from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore (supervised by Prof. Bengang Xing). In 2019-2020, she worked with Prof. Xiaoyuan (Shawn) Chen at National Institutes of Health (NIH) as a postdoctoral fellow. From 2020 to 2025, She worked as an independent principal investigator (PI) at Peking University Shenzhen Hospital. Her current research interests include chemical and biological tools for bioimaging or therapeutics, and smart gene/drug delivery systems for a variety of biomedical applications. To date, Prof. Mu has published more than 60 SCI papers in journals including Nat. Commun., Adv. Mater., Chem. Soc. Rev., Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. , and Adv. Sci. , etc . Her publications have been cited over 3,700 times with an H-index of 29. She also serves as a Youth Editorial Board Member of Exploration , View , and SmartMat , and as Guest Editor for journals such as ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces, Luminescence and Int. J. Mol. Sci.
