## Biography

Lingbo Zhao is an assistant professor in the Division of Global and Area Studies at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received the dual-title Ph.D. in Political Science and Social Data Analytics (SoDA) from the Pennsylvania State University in 2026. Her research focuses on international political economy, particularly on how global supply chains shape political cleavages among firms and influence trade politics. She investigates how global supply chains affect corporate responses to transnational regulations, especially in the areas of climate governance and economic sanctions. Her articles have appeared in the International Studies Quarterly and the Journal of Contemporary China. She also received a B.Soc.Sc in Government and International Studies from Hong Kong Baptist University and an M.Phil. in Politics and Public Administration from the University of Hong Kong.
