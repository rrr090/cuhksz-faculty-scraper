## Biography

Dr. Wu Song is a “Leading Talent in Medicen of Guangdong Province" and "Leading Talent in Science and Technology Innovation of Guangdong Province". He is also the Vice President of Shenzhen Luohu Hospital Group. He has led to host one program in 2017 National Key R&D Program "Stem Cell and Translational Medicine" and 2 in National Natural Science Funds. The research results of his team was published in the international academic journals such as Nature Gen, European Urology and Cell Rer , and he has obtained 4 national invention patents for bladder cancer. He was awarded "The Youth Science and Technology Innovation Talents Plan Award of Guangdong Province", "The 1st prize in Natural Science of Guangdong Province", "Forbes Under 30 Summit-Influential Elite in Asian Medical Treatment and Health", "The 2nd Prize for Outstanding Achievement Award of the Ministry of Education" and "The National May 1st Labor Medal".
