## Biography

Professor GAO Xiaoxue is currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, China. She received her B.Sc. degree from Nanjing University and her Ph.D. degree from the National University of Singapore, with a visiting research appointment at the National Institute of Informatics, Japan. She was previously a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore and a Scientist at A*STAR, Singapore. She also served as an Associate Faculty at the Singapore Institute of Technology, teaching Large Language Models. Her research is internationally well recognized in large audio and speech models, music and singing processing, trustworthy AI, and multilingual and emotional speech technologies. Her work has been published with high impact in leading venues including IEEE TASLP, IEEE TMM, IEEE SPL, TACL, AAAI, ACL, EMNLP, and ICASSP. Professor GAO’s academic leadership extends beyond her research contributions, with active involvement in major conferences, workshops, and challenges in speech, audio, and AI. She is an IEEE Senior Member and serves as an Area Chair for INTERSPEECH 2026, a Senior Area Chair for AACL 2026, an organizer of the Audio-AAAI Workshop at AAAI 2026, an organizer of the TrustAudio Workshop at AACL 2026, and an organizer of the VoiceMOS Challenge 2026.
