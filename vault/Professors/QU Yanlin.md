## Biography

Yanlin Qu is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen in 2026, he was a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in the Decision, Risk, and Operations Division at Columbia Business School, working with Assaf Zeevi and Hongseok Namkoong. He received his Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from Stanford University in 2024, where he was advised by Peter Glynn and Jose Blanchet. He received his B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2018. His research centers on applied probability, particularly at its interface with operations research and machine learning. He currently studies Markov chains and processes, multi-armed bandits, and stochastic simulation. His work has appeared in the Annals of Applied Probability , Queueing Systems , Mathematics , NeurIPS, and ICML. He is the recipient of the 2023 INFORMS Applied Probability Society Best Student Paper Prize.
