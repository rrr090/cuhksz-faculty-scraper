## Biography

Professor Ka Tik CHEUNG holds a PhD in Medical Microbiology from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, a Master of Medical Science (Pathology) from The University of Hong Kong, and a Bachelor of Science in Biological Science with Distinction from Edinburgh Napier University. An accredited Fellow of the Institute of Biomedical Science and an International Technologist in Clinical Chemistry, Professor Cheung's research focuses on zoonotic diseases, antimicrobial resistance, dengue virus, and the application of AI in laboratory medicine. He is an active member of several professional bodies, including the Hong Kong Society for Molecular Diagnostic Sciences and the Hong Kong Institute of Medical Laboratory Sciences.
