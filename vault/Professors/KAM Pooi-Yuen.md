## Biography

Prof. Pooi-Yuen KAM was born in Ipoh, Malaysia, where he had his pre-university education. After receiving his college education at MIT and working for a short period at the Bell Telephone Laboratories, he taught for nearly forty years at the National University of Singapore (NUS) as a professor. He is now a full Professor and the Assistant Dean (Student Affairs) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen.
