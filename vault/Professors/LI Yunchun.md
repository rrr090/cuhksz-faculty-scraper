## Biography

Lecturer Yunchun Li graduated from the University of Yanbian in 2011 and obtained her master’s degree from the University of Qingdao in 2014. She has been worked as a dietitian for 10 years and joined the Second Affiliated Hospital of CUHKSZ in 2023. Her daily job in hospital includes providing nutrition consultation service for outpatients and inpatients, implementing individualized nutritional intervention and she is good at nutrition therapy for diabetes, chronic kidney disease, cancer, obesity. Lecturer Yunchun Li is a member of Chinese Nutrition Society and participated in Youth Committee of Guangdong Nutrition Society in 2023. She is also a specialist of Longgang District Elderly Nutrition Improvement Action Group.
