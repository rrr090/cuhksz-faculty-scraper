## Biography

Lecturer Zhuang graduated from the Mudanjiang Medical University in 2020 and obtained the master of Dermatology from the Guangdong Medical University in 2023. His Standardized resident training was completed in Guangdong Provincial Dermatology Hospital. His research area related to psoriasis, urticaria,skin immunity and bioinformatics analysis. Lecturer Zhuang was a member of Specialized Committee of Traditional Chinese Medicine Cosmetology, Shenzhen Society of Traditional Chinese Medicine from 2023 to 2027. He participated in Professional Committee of Dermatology, Shenzhen Health Management Association from 2024 to 2029 and joined in Shenzhen Association of Integrative Medicine from 2024 to 2029.
