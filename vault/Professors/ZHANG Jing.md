## Biography

Lecturer Zhang graduated from Shanxi Medical University in 2008 with a Master's degree. She joined the Longgang District People's Hospital in the same year and joined the Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2024. Her research interests include asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, pulmonary embolism, pulmonary infections, and sleep apnea syndrome. Lecturer Zhang completed the "Safety, Quality, Informatics, and Leadership Program" at Harvard Medical School from May 19, 2017, to May 13, 2018. Now, Lecturer Zhang is also a member of the Shenzhen Interstitial Lung Disease Study Group.
