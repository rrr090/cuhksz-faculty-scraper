## Biography

Lecturer Zhou graduated from Hubei University of Medicine in 2023 with a Master's degree in General Medicine. In July of the same year, she began working at the Second Affiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where she continues to serve. Her research interests include Chronic Disease Management and Health Education, Primary Prevention at the Community Level, Mental Health Education and Counseling, General Practice Education and Training. Lecturer Zhou currently holds the position of Clinical Skills Trainer at the Second Affiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She is also a faculty member for standardized general practice training at the hospital level and a committee member of the Community Rehabilitation and Chronic Disease Management Special Committee of the Shenzhen Female Physicians Association.
