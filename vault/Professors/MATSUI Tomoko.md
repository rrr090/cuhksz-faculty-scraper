## Biography

Tomoko MATSUI is a Professor at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI), affiliated with the Center for Language, Intelligence and Machines (LIMA). She received her Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Tokyo Institute of Technology in 1997. Previously, she worked at NTT, ATR, and the Institute of Statistical Mathematics (ISM), Japan. Her research interests include statistical machine learning, speech and language processing, multimodal AI, trustworthy AI, and AI for cognitive health and healthy aging. She has published over 100 peer-reviewed papers in leading international journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions, Speech Communication, ICASSP, and Interspeech.
