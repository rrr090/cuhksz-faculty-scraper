## Biography

Dr. Jinsheng Ba is an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He earned his Ph.D. from the National University of Singapore in 2024 and subsequently served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at ETH Zurich. His primary research bridges Software Engineering and Data Science, with a specific focus on observing, manipulating, and shaping software behaviors at a semantic level to enhance the reliability of critical data- and AI-driven systems. Dr. Ba publishes exclusively in premier venues; all of his research has been accepted to top-tier conferences, including ICSE, SIGMOD, and USENIX Security. As a first author, he is the recipient of two ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards at top-tier conferences and was nominated for the Best Ph.D. Thesis Award. His research has led to the discovery of over 200 previously unknown bugs and vulnerabilities in widely used software. He serves on the Program Committees of leading conferences, such as FSE, ASE, ISSTA, and OOPSLA. Dr. Jinsheng Ba has multiple open PhD and MPhil positions for Spring and Fall 2027. Interested candidates are welcome to contact him to apply.
