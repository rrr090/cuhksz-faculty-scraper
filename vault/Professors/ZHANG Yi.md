## Biography

Dr. Yi Zhang is an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2025, advised by Professor Xiaofeng Shao and co-advised by Professor Yun Yang. Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, he was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Washington University in St. Louis. Dr. Zhang's research focuses on reliable statistical inference for complex data, including time series, high-dimensional data, functional data, object-valued data, and image data. His work develops methods for bandwidth-free inference, sample splitting, self-normalization, change-point detection, conditional independence testing, and generative neural network-assisted statistical inference. His papers have appeared or been accepted in journals and conferences including the Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B, Journal of the American Statistical Association, Journal of Business & Economic Statistics, Journal of Time Series Analysis, International Conference on Machine Learning, and SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics.
