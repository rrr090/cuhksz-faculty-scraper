## Biography

Dr. Xiang’s scholarship pursues three lines of research inquiries: (1) the interplay between organizational behavior, social governance and policy impact; (2) the organizational mechanism of nonprofit organizations in empowering community resilience, social equity; (3) the advancement of the interactive dynamic among the country, society and individuals through the relational perspective. His works on these areas have been published in peer-review English and Chinese journals and book. By combining the research experience, Dr. Xiang’s work has also contributed to policy changes for social governance at the municipal level, and enhanced the efficacy of advocacy for a variety of social groups. His ongoing projects deal with different aspects of social governance, such as talent management, institutional integration, education policy development. he is dedicated to the contribution of knowledge exchange activities in international and local settings. He holds a PhD Degree in nonprofit management from the Department of Social Work and Social Administration, University of Hong Kong. Prior to joining School of Public Policy, CUHK (Shenzhen), He had a diversified working experience at different universities, NGOs, and think tanks.
