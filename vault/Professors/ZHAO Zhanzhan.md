## Biography

Dr. Zhanzhan ZHAO is a joint tenure-track assistant professor in both the School of Artificial Intelligence and the Computational Social Science program of the School of Humanities and Social Science. She is also affiliated with the School of Data Science. She is interested in AI for social science, with a focus on algorithmic governance. Her work aims to design public policies driven by computational algorithms that promote transparency and justice, while mitigating local biases and inequality. Her methodological approach includes: Developing sampling methods and AI-assisted tools for policy generation; Leveraging large language model–driven social simulations, complex systems modeling, and network science to analyze and predict social dynamics; Applying game theory and multi-agent reinforcement learning to investigate the interaction between policy interventions and emergent social behavior. She has applied these methods to applications in several areas, including uncovering biases in U.S. election policies, generating equitable school zoning policies, and analyzing health policies related to epidemics. She is also interested in the social science of AI, studying how AI systems reflect, reinforce, or possibility able to be designed to mitigate societal biases and inequalities. Her research explores algorithmic fairness, the societal impact of AI, and the dynamics of human–AI interaction. She is particularly interested in how to design fair and adaptive AI systems that not only make accurate individual-level predictions but also fair across various demographic groups, and promote collective welfare. Several applications include fairly predicting students’ performance in college education systems, and developing mechanism designs that support individual creativity while improving LLM performance, as well as preserving collective language diversity. Dr. Zhao is always open to collaborate with students, researchers, and practitioners. Feel free to reach out to her for any inquiries or potential collaborations.
