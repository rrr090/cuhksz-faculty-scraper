## Biography

YANG Yong is an Adjunct Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, while concurrently serving as an External Graduate Supervisor at Tsinghua University, Deputy Director of the Peking University Laboratory of China Environment for Network Innovations, and an Executive Committee Member of the CCF Technical Committee on Network and System Security. Prof. YANG possesses extensive experience in architecture design, engineering implementation, and product development across application and operations security, business security, and information security. In recent years, his research has focused on attacks and defenses for LLMs, achieving a series of innovative results in LLM infrastructure security, intrinsic security, and AI for cybersecurity. He has received multiple public acknowledgments from leading global technology companies including NVIDIA, Google, and Microsoft. His academic work has been published in top conferences such as ICLR, CVPR, and NeurIPS, covering key AI security topics such as dataset copyright protection as well as backdoor attacks and defenses. He has also led or participated in national-level security assurance projects and National Key R&D Program projects, proposing solutions to core technological bottlenecks.
