## Biography

Dr. Evelyn Li graduated with a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Medical Sciences and Biology from Western University, Canada, in 2011 and earned her Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) from the American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine in 2015. She is a surgeon uniquely qualified with dual training, having successfully completed a surgical residency in Beijing after passing both the United States Medical Licensing Examinations (USMLE) and the Chinese National Medical Licensing Examination. This pathway has given her firsthand experience in bridging the transition from a Western, English-language medical training system to clinical practice within the Chinese healthcare system. The story of Paul Shu-Pei Tien inspired Dr. Li's medical career. Tien, a fellow Tianjin native, founded the American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine (AUC) in 1978. His vision was to offer an innovative, global, American-style education to Chinese immigrants when access to such programs was limited. She is particularly passionate about leveraging this experience through innovative teaching methods, such as comprehensive English-based medical education, to cultivate a new generation of doctors who are adaptable and proficient in both international and domestic medical contexts. Her clinical training included rotations at prestigious U.S. institutions, and she has actively contributed to research at the forefront of medical AI, co-developing diagnostic platforms and contributing to the MedGPT project. Her current work at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, integrates her clinical expertise in surgery with her commitment to advancing medical education and technological integration in healthcare.
