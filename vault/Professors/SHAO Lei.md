## Biography

Adjunct Clinical Lecturer Shao Lei graduated from Henan University Medical School in 2006 and Guangdong Medical University in 2009. He has previously studied at the Seventh People's Hospital in Shanghai, the Affiliated Hospital of Guangdong Medical University, the Shao Yi Fu Hospital, and the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University. Adjunct Clinical Lecturer Shao has been working at Longgang District People's Hospital in Shenzhen since 2009, where he has been engaged in nephrology and vascular access-related work since his employment. He has treated over 17,000 outpatients and over 3,500 inpatients. He has independently completed over 1,000 operations and high-risk procedures, including long-term and temporary hemodialysis catheters, fistula formation, self-expandable vascular access balloon angioplasty (PTA), central venous PTA, hemodialysis and CRRT. He has also published numerous articles on this field. Academic Appointments: Member of the Kidney Disease Rehabilitation Special Committee of the Chinese Rehabilitation Medicine Association, Member of the Young Scholars Group of the Kidney Disease Rehabilitation Special Committee of the Chinese Rehabilitation Medicine Association, Member of the Chinese Medical Association's Nephrology Rehabilitation Special Committee, Member of the First Committee of the Vascular Access Special Committee of the Guangdong Health Management Association, Member of the Vascular Access Special Committee of the Guangdong Medical Industry Association, Member of the Vascular Access Subcommittee of the Shenzhen Nephrologists Association, Member of the Shenzhen Medical Association's Nephrology Special Committee, Member of the Shenzhen Nephrologists Association's Second Council, Member of the Kidney Disease Prevention and Rehabilitation Special Committee of the Shenzhen Health Management Association, and Member of the Vascular Access Group.
