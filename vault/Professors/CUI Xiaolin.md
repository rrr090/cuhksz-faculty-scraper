## Biography

As a professional biochemical engineer, Dr. Cui joined the University of Otago as a postdoctoral Fellow after completing his PhD from the University of Adelaide in 2017. He was later promoted to Research Fellow in 2019. In 2021, he was appointed as an adjunct associate professor at the Department of Bone and Joint, The First Affiliated Hospital of Dalian Medical University. In July 2022, he joined the School of Medicine as an Assistant Professor at CUHKSZ. Dr. Cui specializes in the interface of stem cells, biomaterials, biofabrication, and tissue engineering. He has a special interest in cartilage and bone regeneration, as well as cardiovascular disease diagnosis and treatment. His expertise includes polymer synthesis, tissue culture, anticancer drug screening, drug and gene delivery systems, and stem cell therapy. Currently, he is leading several pre-clinical trials in the field of tissue engineering. Dr. Cui has received the 2021 WFIRM Young Investigator Award from the International Society for Biofabrication. Additionally, he has received a provisional PhD scholarship from the University of Adelaide, University of Otago Divisional Health Science Postdoctoral Fellowship, National Heart Foundation of New Zealand Research Fellowship, New Zealand Ministry for Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE) Science Whitinga fellowship, and New Zealand Foundational Advice Emerging Researcher Award. As a Principal Investigator (PI), Dr. Cui has also received an Explorer Grant from the Health Research Council and a major project grant from the Heart Foundation。 Dr. Cui is a member of the Royal Society of New Zealand (RSNZ), the Royal Society of Chemistry, the American Chemical Society, the International Society for Biofabrication (ISBF), and is the emerging researcher representative (NZ) of the Australasian Society for Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering (ASBTE). He represented New Zealand at the 2022 Global Young Scientists Summit. Currently, he serves as an associate editor of Frontiers in Biomaterials Science and is a part of the early career researcher (ECR) editorial boards of Exploration (Weily), Military Medical Research (BMC, Spring Nature), and Extracellular Vesicles and Circulating Nucleic Acids (EVCNA). He has also acted as a guest editor for Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology. Dr. Cui is an invited reviewer for over 20 SCI index journals, including Science Advances, Advanced Materials, Advanced Functional Materials, Bioactive Material, Small, Advanced Healthcare Materials, and Acta Biomaterialia, among others.
