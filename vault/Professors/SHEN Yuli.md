## Biography

Shen Yuli graduated from Guangzhou Medical University with Master’s degree of internal medicine, and joined the second affiliated hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2015. She had worked as a visiting fellow in Department of Nephrology, Klinikum rechts der Isar, Technische Universitat München for one year(2020). Her research area related to chronic kidney diseases, hyperuricemia and immune dysfunction of hemodialysis. She is the youth member of Plasma Purification Committee of Guangdong Clinical Association and Nephrology Committee of Shenzhen Medical Association. She is the secretary of nephrology committee of Longgang Medical Association.
