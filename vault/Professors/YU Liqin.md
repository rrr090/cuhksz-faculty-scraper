## Biography

Lecturer Yu Liqin graduated from Guangdong Medical College in 2015 and 2018. From 2018 to 2021, she completed standardized training for general practitioner residents in Shenzhen Grade 3 Hospital. From 2021 to now, Lecturer Yu Liqin has been engaged in clinical services in general practice in grassroots institutions affiliated to Shenzhen Longgang District People's Hospital. During the period, she participated in Shenzhen Senior Family Doctor training Course and Shenzhen General Practitioner Psychiatric sub-specialty training, and constantly improved her knowledge and skills. Lecturer Yu served as a member of the Metabolic Disease Prevention and Control Professional Committee of Shenzhen Preventive Medicine Association, and a member of the Obesity and Weight Management Professional Committee of Shenzhen Sino-Western Association. In 2023, she won the second prize in Shenzhen Skills Competition - Grassroots Health post training and Skills Competition, and was included in the 2024 "Shenzhen Craftsman" cultivation plan
