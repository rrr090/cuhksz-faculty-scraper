## Biography

Liu Zaiyi， Doctor of Medicine, winner of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars; Chief Physician, Professor, Doctoral Supervisor; Harvard University Visiting Scholar. Director of Department of Radiology, Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital. Dr. Liu has been committed to medical image big data mining, imaging artificial intelligence research, and published more than 30 SCI papers with the first and corresponding authors, including J Clin Oncol, Ann Oncol, Clin Cancer Res and Radiology. He has hosted over a number of national-level projects including the the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars and the National Key R&D Program of China. Dr Liu has obtained 1 national invention patent, 1 utility model patent, and 4 computer software copyright registrations. His outstanding work in the research of imaging big data has promoted the development of imaging research in the country to a certain extent, and has been invited to give special reports at international academic conferences, including the World Molecular Imaging Conference and the European Radiology Conference.
