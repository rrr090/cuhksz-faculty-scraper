## Biography

Dr. Galymzhan Kuatbay graduated from Nazarbayev University with a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Biological Sciences in 2015 and obtained his Doctor of Medicine (MD) degree from Nazarbayev University School of Medicine (NUSOM) in 2019. His clinical training included placements at Joint Commission International (JCI)-accredited medical centers. In 2019, he gained clinical exposure to the US healthcare system at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC) Presbyterian Hospital in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He subsequently completed his residency in Internal Medicine at NUSOM in 2025. Following his residency, Dr. Kuatbay worked as an Internal Medicine physician in the Emergency Care/Admission Department at the Medical Center Hospital of the President’s Affairs Administration in Astana, Kazakhstan, before joining the School of Medicine at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Alongside his clinical practice, he has experience in medical education, having served as a Clinical Preceptor at NUSOM from 2021 to 2025. In this role, he conducted bedside teaching, facilitated case-based discussions, and helped medical students develop clinical reasoning skills. Dr. Kuatbay’s research interests include diabetes prevention, early intervention, and evidence-based approaches to improving patient outcomes. His clinical experience managing critically ill patients with diabetic ketoacidosis has motivated his interest in diabetes care and patient education, particularly dietary modification, evidence-based treatment, and the prevention of diabetes-related complications. He is ECFMG-certified and speaks Kazakh, Russian, English, and Turkish.
