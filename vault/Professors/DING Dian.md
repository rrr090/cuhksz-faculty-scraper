## Biography

Prof. Dian Ding obtained her Bachelor's degree in Bioengineering from Jilin University in 2015 and completed her Ph.D. in Integrative Life Sciences (Biology) at Peking University in 2022. She subsequently pursued postdoctoral research at Peking University Health Science Center. She focused on structural biology approaches to study membrane protein systems.
