## Biography

Prof. Chan holds a doctorate from Monash University, Australia. He conducted research at the Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre and completed postdoctoral training at The University of Hong Kong (HKU). Prof. Chan subsequently dedicated approximately 20 years to HKU’s LKS Faculty of Medicine in academic roles involving teaching, PhD/MPhil supervision, and laboratory management. His career also includes leadership as Senior Vice-President at an international biotech company, focusing on cell therapy development and GMP manufacturing, and a role at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)'s School of Biomedical Sciences. He is currently an Associate Professor at CUHK-Shenzhen’s School of Medicine, a position he has held since joining in 2022, where he teaches in the clinical medicine programme. His research focuses on the tumor microenvironment, investigating its role in tumor growth, metastasis, and chemoresistance. Utilizing clinical data, cellular and animal models, and bioinformatics, his work deciphers signaling pathways involved in tumor-stromal interactions, onco-immunology, and cancer metabolism.
