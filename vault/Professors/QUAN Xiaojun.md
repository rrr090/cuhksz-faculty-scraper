## Biography

QUAN Xiaojun is an Adjunct Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen and a Professor at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong and was a visiting scholar at Rutgers Business School and the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University. He previously held positions at City University of Hong Kong, the Institute for Infocomm Research (I²R), A*STAR, Singapore, and the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University. His research interests include on-device AI models, large language models, model fusion, and model compression and efficiency. His current research focuses on on-device speech agents, heterogeneous model fusion, and the alignment and compression of small language models. He has published more than 100 papers in leading journals and conferences, including IEEE T-PAMI, ACL, ICML, NeurIPS, and ICLR. His team was among the first to explore multi-source heterogeneous large language model fusion and has developed a series of models, including FuseLLM and FuseChat.
