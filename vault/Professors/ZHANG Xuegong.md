## Biography

ZHANG Xuegong is currently a Visiting Professor at School of Artificial Intelligence of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his B.S. degree in Industry Automation from Tsinghua University in 1989, and his Ph.D. degree in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems in 1994. He joined the faculty of Tsinghua University in the Department of Automation from April 1994, where he served as a Lecturer, Associate Professor and Professor. He had worked as a visiting scientist at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health in 2001-2002 and 2006, and at University of Southern California in 2007. He is currently Professor of Pattern Recognition and Bioinformatics in the Department of Automation and Beijing Research Center for Information Science and Technology (BNRIST), and Adjunct Professor of the School of Life Sciences and School of Medicine. He also serves as a member of the Faculty Committee of the Department of Statistics and Data Science, and that of the Vanke School of Public Health. His work has been published in high-impact journals such as Nature Methods, Nature Machine Intelligence, Nature Medicine, Nature Communications, Cell Stem Cell, National Science review, Bioinformatics, Nucleic Acid Research, Genome Biology, Journal of Machine Learning Research, The Lancet Digital Health, etc. His book Pattern Recognition (2nd-5th Edition) has been wide used as the textbook in many universities in China. He was elected as Fellow of the International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB) and Fellow of the Chinese Association of Artificial Intelligence (CAAI) in 2020, and was elected as the Vice President of ISCB in 2024. He is also the Chair of the Committee for Intelligent Health and Bioinformatics in the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA). He is the co-Editor-in-Chief of the journal Quantitative Biology .
