## Biography

Prof. YU is currently an Associate Professor and Presidential Young Fellow jointly appointed by the School of Science and Engineering and the School of Artificial Intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen. He is also the Director of Microrobotics Center, Shenzhen Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society (AIRS). He is the academic leader in China Merchants Group-AIRS Joint Lab for Future Medical Robotics. His research interests mainly focus on micro/nanorobotics, including their modelling, actuation, control and medical applications. He has published over 90 top journal and international conference papers, including Nature Machine Intelligence, Nature Biomedical Engineering, Science Advances, Nature Communications, IJRR, IEEE TRO and Advanced Materials. Four papers have been indexed as ESI highly cited papers, and have been highlighted in Science, Nature and international press reports, such as CNN. He has also published 2 monographs and 1 edited book. He is an IEEE Senior Member, and has received impactful awards, including IEEE RAS Early Academic Career Award, MIT TR35 Asia Pacific, IROS Best Student Conference Paper, Frontiers of Science Award on International Congress of Basic Science, IEEE 3M-NANO Rising Star Award, Wu Wen Jun AI Science & Technology Award, Baidu Global Chinese Young Scholar in AI, and T-Mech Best Paper Award Finalist. He serves in the editorial board of IEEE TRO and TASE, and as a reviewer for top journals and conferences, including Science Robotics, Nature Nanotechnology, Nature Sensors, Nature Electronics, Science Advances, Nature Communications and PNAS.
