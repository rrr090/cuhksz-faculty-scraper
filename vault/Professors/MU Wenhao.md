## Biography

MU Wenhao, a young conductor and music scholar, is currently a faculty member at the School of Music, CUHK-Shenzhen. In 2022, he received his Doctoral degree in Conducting from the Eastman School of Music, University of Rochester, with a full scholarship, where he studied under Professors William Weinert and Brad Lubman and served as assistant conductor for the school's orchestras and choirs. He earned his Master's degree in Choral Conducting from Brigham Young University in 2019. He has also studied symphonic and operatic conducting under distinguished maestros including Zheng Xiaoying, Neil Varon, Johannes Wildner, among others. From 2023 to 2026, he served as Principal Conductor of the Shenzhen Opera and Dance Theatre, leading the chorus through over 80 performances and international tours in Southeast Asia and Europe. He prepared and conducted major productions such as Die Zauberflöte, Don Giovanni, Carmen, Carmina Burana, and the Andrea Bocelli Gala Concert in Macau. In 2024, he won the Gold Award at the Austrian Brahms International Orchestra Conducting Competition and was appointed as a member of the Artistic Committee of the World Orchestra Festival. In 2019, he was shortlisted among the top 30 (Judges' Commendation List) in the Hong Kong International Choral Conducting Competition, being the only contestant from mainland China selected. In 2017, the International Choral Union honored him with the "Cultural Ambassador of Choral Music" title. In 2021, he guest-conducted the Chongqing Opera House Symphony Orchestra for the symphonic choral concert tour Ode to the Sun . In 2020, he served as the overall director of two benefit concerts at the Eastman School of Music in support of Wuhan. As a music scholar, he has written program notes and delivered pre-concert lectures for the Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra and other ensembles. He co-translated the medieval section of DK Publishing's Music: The Definitive Visual History (published in 2023 by Encyclopedia of China Publishing House). He has given academic lectures in Malta, Laos, and Thailand, served as a guest clinician for nearly 100 ensembles worldwide, and presented lectures or provided on-site interpretation for foreign experts at over 40 international music festivals and conferences, including the 2026 World Choral Music Symposium in Macau. Earlier in his career, he earned both a Bachelor's and a Master's degree in Urban Planning and Design from Tianjin University, where he was active in the university's arts ensembles, serving as principal percussionist, student assistant conductor of the Peiyang Symphony Orchestra, and student assistant conductor of the Peiyang Choir. In 2012, while pursuing his Master's degree, he participated in Professor Zheng Xiaoying's conducting class and was voted "Outstanding Participant" by all members of the Xiamen Philharmonic Orchestra.
