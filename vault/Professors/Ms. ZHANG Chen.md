## Biography

ZHANG Chen is a Collaborative Piano faculty member at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her early professional piano training at the Music Middle Schools Affiliated to Shanghai Conservatory of Music, studying with Professor Zhang Chongfang, renowned composer Wang Jianzhong, and Professor William Chen Weiling. She later pursued advanced studies in the United Kingdom. She graduated from the Royal Northern College of Music (RNCM) and the Royal College of Music (RCM), where she obtained her Bachelor of Music (Honours) in Piano Performance, a Master of Music in Piano Performance, and a Postgraduate Performance Diploma. She further advanced her professional training at Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance in London. During her overseas studies, she studied under distinguished pedagogues including Benjamin Frith, Gold Medalist of the Arthur Rubinstein International Piano Master Competition, as well as Professors Andrew Zolinsky and Elena Riu. Her competitive accolades include First Prize at the RNCM Piano Duo Competition, First Prize (Teachers’ Category) at the 12th China-Shenzhen Piano Duo and Four-Hand Piano Invitational Competition, and the Commended Performance Award at the John Halford Competition (Composition and Piano). As an active solo and collaborative pianist internationally, her performance credits include the Chopin 200th Anniversary Piano Festival Concert Series, Messiaen Piano Works Concert Series, London Borough New Music Concert Series, Shanghai Spring International Music Festival, Lü Siqing-Shenzhen Futian International String Art Week, and 2025 Shenzhen Belt and Road International Music Festival. She has also been repeatedly invited to perform in concerts hosted by the Chinese Consulate General in Manchester. She has collaborated with world-renowned musicians, including Lü Siqing, Vera Tsu Weiling, Mark Messenger and Sophie Rowell. She regularly works as a professional collaborative pianist for major international competitions, including the Hong Kong International Violin Competition, Hong Kong International Chamber Music Competition, and Futian Cup International Youth String Elite Competition. Equally devoted to music education and artistic coaching, she has guided numerous students to win awards in national and international solo and chamber music competitions.
