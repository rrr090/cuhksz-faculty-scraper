## Biography

Dr. Jihang Chen obtained his Ph.D. in Biochemistry from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He is currently a Presidential Young Scholar and independent Principal Investigator at the School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen), where he also serves as a supervisor for Ph.D. students and postdoctoral researchers. He has been selected for the Guangdong Province Young Top-Notch Talent Program and the Shenzhen Peacock Plan High-Level Talent Program, and is a recipient of the CUHK-Shenzhen Presidential Exemplary Teaching Award. Dr. Chen’s research focuses on the pharmacological activities, mechanisms of action, and bioactive material basis of traditional Chinese medicine and natural products, as well as food and nutrition. In particular, his work emphasizes the mechanisms by which medicinal and food homologous Chinese medicines ameliorate diabetes and its complications. He has extensive experience in the analysis and pharmacology of traditional Chinese medicine, as well as in the development and evaluation of functional foods for health promotion. Dr. Chen has led more than 10 research projects, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China Youth and General Programs, Guangdong Province General Program, Shenzhen Municipal General Program, international collaborative projects, and research projects in partnership with enterprises and public institutions. He serves as Associate Editor of Phytomedicine and Phytochemicals in Food and Medicine , Young Editorial Board Member of eFood and Chinese Medicine , and Guest Editor for international academic journals including Phytotherapy Research, Journal of Food Biochemistry , and Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine (eCAM) .
