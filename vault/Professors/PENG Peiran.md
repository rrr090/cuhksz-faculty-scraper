## Biography

Dr. PENG Peiran is an Assistant Professor (Teaching) in the Division of Language Education. She teaches ENG1001, ENG1002, and courses in English literature. Her research brings 4E cognition into dialogue with canonical nineteenth-century English fiction, examining how physical environments participate in the construction of characters’ inner realities and sense of self. In pedagogical innovation, she explores how literature can enrich whole-person education, with a particular focus on developing the humanistic literacy, empathy, and critical creativity of students from STEM backgrounds. Her research has been published in Journal of Victorian Culture .
