## Biography

Professor Sha graduated from West China School of Stomatology, Sichuan University (formerly the School of Stomatology, West China University of Medical Sciences), and obtained her Bachelor's Degree in Stomatology. Immediately after graduation, she served as Resident and Doctor-in-charge in the Department of Stomatology, Beijing Tiantan Hospital. She later pursued advanced studies at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where she was awarded Master's and Doctoral Degrees in Anatomy. She once worked as a Teaching Assistant and Research Associate in the Department of Anatomy, Faculty of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, with extensive experience in anatomical teaching and scientific research. From 2009 to 2026, she held the positions of Professor of Histology and Embryology, Doctoral Supervisor, and Class B Talent of the "Pengcheng Peacock" Program in Shenzhen University Medical School. She successively served as the Chairperson of the Department of Anatomy, Histology and Embryology in the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Associate Dean of the School of Dentistry of the Medical School, and Deputy Director of the Institute of Stomatology of Shenzhen University. She is the founder of the English-taught program for Histology and Embryology at Shenzhen University, as well as the founder of the Dentistry discipline and the School of Dentistry of Shenzhen University. Professor Sha joined the School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in September 2026. Her academic part-time appointments cover a wide range of prestigious domestic and international roles, including Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (UK), Honorary Professor of the University of Hong Kong, Member of the Medical Ethics Committee at the Shenzhen Research Institute of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Expert for Undergraduate Education Evaluation of National Ordinary Institutions of Higher Learning, Executive Director of the Alliance for First-Class Classroom Design and Practice in Medical Universities under the Joint Council of National Experimental Teaching Demonstration Centers for Higher Education, Executive Member of the Histology and Embryology Branch of the Chinese Society for Anatomical Sciences, Executive Member of the Translational Medicine Branch of the Chinese Medical Biotechnology Association, Executive Member of the Joint Council of Directors of National Basic Medical Morphology Laboratories and Former Chairman of its Histology and Embryology Branch, Editorial Board Member of Chinese Journal of Histochemistry and Cytochemistry , Editorial Board Member of Chinese Journal of Stomatological Research , Vice Chairman of the Medical Education Branch of the Guangdong Translational Medicine Association, Vice President of the Shenzhen Alumni Association of West China School of Stomatology at Sichuan University, and Executive Director of the Shenzhen Biomedical Promotion Association. Professor Sha has been awarded the title of "Excellent Instructor" in the National Medical Innovation Competition for College Students & the "Belt and Road" International Competition Finals by the Medical Innovation Competition Committee for College Students, "Shenzhen Outstanding Teacher" by Shenzhen Municipal Education Bureau, as well as multiple honors from Shenzhen University, including "Excellent Master Supervisor of Shenzhen University", Instructor of the "First Batch of Top 100 Outstanding Master's Degree Theses of Shenzhen University", "Excellent Instructor for College Students' Innovation Training Program", "Teaching Achievement Award of Shenzhen University", and "Special Contribution Award of Shenzhen University Medical School". She has presided over more than 20 research and educational reform projects at the national, provincial and municipal levels, obtained 2 invention patents, published over 20 Chinese and English textbooks/monographs, and released more than 100 papers indexed in SCI and Chinese core journals.
