## Biography

Professor Zhiguo Zhou joined the School of Medicine at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in June 2026. Since December 2014, he worked as a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Radiation Oncology at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center, and was promoted to Research Instructor in September 2017. From 2019 to 2022, he served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Cybersecurity, University of Central Missouri. Then he moved to School of Medicine, University of Kansas as an Assistant Professor from 2022 to 2026. He has published more than 100 papers, with more 2,700 citations. His current research interests include reliable AI, AI in clinical medicine, machine learning and deep learning, radiomics, treatment outcome prediction, clinical decision support, medical image processing, pathological analysis, and related areas.
