## Biography

Prof. Hwang is a Senior Consultant Anaesthesiologist with Department of Anaesthesiology, Division of Anaesthesiology & Perioperative Medicine in Singapore General Hospital (SGH), and with the Department of Cardiothoracic Anaesthesia, National Heart Centre Singapore, Singapore Healthcare Services. I served as the Head of Department of Cardiothoracic Anaesthesia in NHCS between May 2003 and April 2017. I am a regular rank Professor in the Office of Medical Education in Duke-NUS Medical School, Adjunct Associate Professor in Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine (YLLSoM) in National University of Singapore, and Clinical Teacher in Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine in National Technological University. I currently contribute as an Executive member of the Asian Society of Cardiovascular Anesthesiologists, a member of the Advisory Group for World Federation of Society of Anaesthesiologists. I serve on the editorial board of Journal of Cardiothoracic and Vascular Anesthesia and review submissions for various journals.
