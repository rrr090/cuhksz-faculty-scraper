## Biography

Dr. Wenbo Chen graduated from The Third Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University in 2018, obtaining a Doctor of Medicine degree in Clinical Medicine (8-year Program), specializing in Medical lmaging and Nuclear Medicine. He is a member of the Ultrasound Medicine Division ofthe Guangdong Primary Medical Association. From 2018 to 2023, Dr. Chen worked in the Ultrasound Department of Qingyuan People's Hospital affiliated with Guangzhou Medical University, where he gained extensive clinical experience and hosted several research projects. In 2023, he joined the Ultrasound Department of The Second Afiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, serving as an Attending Physician and Adjunct Lecturer, engaging in clinical practice and academic teaching. Dr. Chen specializes in ultrasound diagnostics of the abdomen, cardiovascular system, and superficial organs. He is proficient in ultrasound-guided interventional technigues, having completed over 200 interventional procedures. His research focuses on the application of ultrasound imaging technology in disease diagnosis and treatment, particularly integrating deeplearning with ultrasound elastography for assessing hepatic fibrosis and diagnosing breast andthyroid diseases. Awards First Prize in the "Spark Cup" Case Competition organized by the Ultrasound Medicine Divisionof the Guangdong Primary Medical Association. Third Place (Second Prize) in the Case Competition organized by the Shenzhen UltrasoundMedical Engineering Society.
