## Biography

Prof. Michael Junhao Qiu is a GMC-licensed doctor and healthcare innovator. He earned a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Medicine from the University of St Andrews and his medical degree (MBChB) from the University of Edinburgh. He completed his postgraduate training at the University Hospital Southampton in Hampshire, England, United Kingdom. Driven by a passion for enhancing patient care through technology, he co-founded the Canadian AI startup JustPractice Technologies, which specializes in transforming Electronic Medical Records (EMR) data into actionable clinical insights to identify care gaps and improve outcomes. His entrepreneurial success is evidenced by securing multiple contracts with international pharmaceutical companies and provincial government health authorities, including Doctors of BC in British Columbia, Canada. The AI-powered tool is now used nationwide across Canada, enhancing physician care and optimizing the use of guideline-recommended medications. Prof. Qiu continues to develop AI-enhanced medical education tools to optimize clinical training. Prof. Qiu’s philosophy is that it is the responsibility of clinicians to foster clinical excellence and continued development in medical education. He is an active advocate within the UK education system, having established the Kirsty Dundas Scholarship in the Future Medicine: Clinical Education Scholarship programme for students at the University of Edinburgh Medical School. As a distinguished alumnus of the University of Edinburgh, he is also a Fellow of the Royal Society of Medicine in the United Kingdom (FRSM). He continues to bring this mindset to develop the newest generation of globally competent medical students at CUHK Shenzhen through career development and student guidance.
