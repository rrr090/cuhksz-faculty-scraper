## Biography

XU Siyuan holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from The Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. ZHU Minghui. He received his B.E. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2019. His research centers on optimization, reinforcement learning, meta-learning, and large language model post-training, with applications in robotics, natural language processing, and cybersecurity. His work has appeared in leading venues including ICML, NeurIPS, EMNLP, AAAI, RSS, ACC, TMLR, Machine Learning, Automatica, and IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control.
