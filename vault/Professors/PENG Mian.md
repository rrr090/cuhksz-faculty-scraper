## Biography

Professor Peng graduated from the University of Guangzhou Medical University and obtained her M.D. from Southern Medical University ( Supervisor: Professor Weiqin Li, Chair of PLA Critical Care Medicine Association ). She joined The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2026. Her research area is related to the sepsis gut-lung axis and simulation-based education. Professor Peng has published on these areas. Professor Peng is the National Standing Member of the Specialized Medical Working Committee, Chinese Medicine Education Association; the National Member of the Infectious Disease Branch, China Association of Chinese Medicine; the Vice Chairman of the Critical and Emergency Committee, Guangdong Elder Health Care Association; the Vice Chairman of the Nutrition Committee, Guangdong Provincial Association of Primary Medicine; the Standing Committee Member of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Guangdong Provincial Association of Chinese Medicine; the Committee Member of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Guangdong Medical Association; the Committee Member of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Guangdong Medical Doctor Association; the Vice Chairman of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Shenzhen Medical Association; the Vice Chairman of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Shenzhen Medical Doctor Association；and the Vice Chairman of the Critical Care Medicine Branch, Shenzhen Chinese Medicine Association.
