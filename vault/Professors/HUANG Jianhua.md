## Biography

Prof. HUANG Jianhua served as an Assistant Professor at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, and later as an Associate Professor and Professor at Texas A&M University. In 2017, he was appointed as the Arseven/Mitchell Astronomical Statistics Chair Professor in the College of Science at Texas A&M University. His administrative roles included Director of the Graduate Program in the Department of Statistics, Acting Associate Dean of the College of Science, Acting Department Head of Statistics, and Deputy Director of the Institute for Data Science. He is a Fellow of the American Statistical Association and the Institute of Mathematical Statistics, as well as an Elected Member of the International Statistical Institute. During his tenure in the U.S., he mentored over 20 doctoral students. He participated in multiple U.S. National Science Foundation projects, served on editorial boards of international journals, and published over 100 research papers in international academic journals. He has been consistently ranked among the top 2% most-cited scientists globally for many years. In 2024, he was recognized on Research.com’s Top Scientists List, named among the Best Scientists in Computer Science and Best Scientists in Mathematics. He was awarded the 2022 INFORMS Impact Prize by the Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences (INFORMS).
