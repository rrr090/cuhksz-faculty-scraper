## Biography

Lecturer Guo graduated from Guangzhou Medical University. After graduation, He worked in the Department of Gastroenterology of The Second Affiliated Hospital of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. His main research is gut microbiota. As a physician in the Gastrointestinal Microecology Center, he is good at using fecal microbiota transplantation to treat patient for various types of functional gastrointestinal diseases, inflammatory bowel diseases, etc.
