## Biography

Dr. Jiang Zhang is currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and has long been devoted to immunology research. She received her B.S. degree from Zhejiang Normal University in 2013 and her M.S. degree from East China Normal University in 2016, and obtained her Ph.D. in Biology from the École Normale Supérieure de Lyon (ENS-Lyon) in 2020. Following her doctoral studies, she pursued postdoctoral training in Dr. Thomas S. Kupper's laboratory at Harvard Medical School, where she participated in and led research on cutaneous memory T cell responses, and collaborated with neuroimmunologist Dr. Isaac M. Chiu to explore the interactions between the nervous and immune systems. Her research findings have been published as first author or co-corresponding author in journals including Journal of Experimental Medicine , Cellular and Molecular Immunology , Nature Communications , and Brain, Behavior, and Immunity . She has received multiple awards at high-level international academic conferences and has been invited to give presentations at Keystone Symposia, Gordon Research Conferences, and the annual meeting of the Society for Investigative Dermatology (SID). In addition, she serves as an independent reviewer for journals including Cellular & Molecular Immunology , Frontiers in Immunology , Communications Biology , and European Journal of Immunology . In October 2026, Dr. Zhang joined the School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where she established her independent research group. Her research focuses on the regulation of immune memory by cutaneous nociceptive sensory neurons. By integrating cutting-edge approaches including single-cell omics, spatial omics, and epigenetics, her lab will explore the fundamental principles of skin-neuron-immune interactions and reveal their roles in skin homeostasis, immunological aging, and disease pathogenesis, with the aim of providing new theoretical insights and intervention strategies for promoting skin health, enhancing protective immunity, and preventing and treating disease.
