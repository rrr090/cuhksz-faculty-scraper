## Biography

Zehao WANG (b. 2000), serving as an Assistant Professor at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, is a music researcher and practitioner whose work explores the relationship between music and technology, with a particular focus on computer-based approaches. His research ranges from technical subjects—musical acoustics, sound synthesis, instrument design, room acoustics simulation and spatial reproduction—to conceptual and critical inquiry, while his creative practice encompasses theatrical projects, compositions, and installations. He has presented his research at institutions including the Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics (CCRMA) at Stanford University, the National University of Singapore, and Peking University. His sound installations and musical works have been exhibited and performed at venues in China and abroad, including the Australian National University, the MISA Festival presented by the Shanghai Symphony Orchestra, Shanghai Concert Hall, Zhao-Moon Art Museum, and Theater for the New City in New York. A committed advocate for education in contemporary music and technology, he has organized and contributed to outreach programs for students from middle school through college at Shanghai Concert Hall, the Shanghai Symphony Orchestra, the X-Institute in Shenzhen, and the COSMOS program of the University of California. Zehao holds a Ph.D. in computer music from the Department of Music at the University of California, San Diego. He has conducted research internships at Microsoft, ByteDance/TikTok, Yamaha, and Apple, and held visiting positions at NYU Shanghai and Stanford's CCRMA. Before his career in music, he earned his bachelor's degree in Mathematics with a concentration in data science from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University in 2021, where he was recognized as an Outstanding Graduate.
