## Biography

LIU Hui is currently an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her B.S. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Xidian University in 2004 and 2008, respectively. Then she earned her second M.S. degree and Ph.D. degree in Biostatistics and Data Science from University of Kansas in 2025 and 2026, respectively. She also worked as Software Engineer at Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. and The University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center for a couple of years. Before she joined CUHK-Shenzhen, she was an Assistant Instructor in Department of Computer Science and Cybersecurity at University of Central Missouri. She has rich industry experience and developed multiple systems independently. Several systems have been used in clinical practice. She also published multiple high-impact journal and conference papers. She received the Best in Physics Award from the American Association of Physicists in Medicine (AAPM) in 2019.
