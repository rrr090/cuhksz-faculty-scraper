## Biography

LEUNG Bo-Wah is currently a Professor of Musicology at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He previously served as a Professor and Head of the Department of Cultural and Creative Arts at The Education University of Hong Kong, as Deputy Dean of the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences, and as Executive Director of the Centre for Xiqu and Intangible Cultural Heritage. He is an internationally recognized authority in research on music education and traditional performing arts. He was the first Chinese President of the International Society for Music Education (ISME) and an Executive Member of the World Alliance for Arts Education (WAAE). He currently serves on the Executive Board of the International Music Council (IMC) as Treasurer and chairs the Asia-Pacific Symposium for Music Education Research (APSMER). In addition, he is a member of the China Musicians Association and the Chinese Quyi Artists Association. His visiting professorships include Chair Professor at Soochow University and Visiting Professor at Shenzhen University, as well as Adjunct Professor at Guangzhou University. In public service, Professor LEUNG serves as a member of the Arts Development Advisory Committee under the Hong Kong Government; Vice Chairman of the Hong Kong–Taiwan Economic and Cultural Cooperation Promotion Association; a Panel Member for the University Grants Committee's Research Assessment Exercise 2026; and an education and disciplinary expert with the Hong Kong Council for Accreditation of Academic and Vocational Qualifications. He is also a consultant to the Music Office and to the School of Chinese Opera, Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts. He has served as a doctoral examiner for universities in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia, and regions such as Hong Kong (China). His work has had a far-reaching impact on global arts and cultural education. For many years, Professor LEUNG has devoted himself to promoting the inheritance of Cantonese opera (Yueju), a World Intangible Cultural Heritage, through primary and secondary school education. The "Chinese Opera—Cantonese Opera Teaching Programme for Primary and Secondary Schools," led by him, received the Musical Rights Award from the International Music Council (IMC)—an organization founded by UNESCO—in 2011. He also led the development of the "Yuequ Accomp" iPad application, the only one patented by the Intellectual Property Department of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, supporting the integration of arts and technology. In academic research, Professor LEUNG has received multiple research grants from the University Grants Committee and the Education Bureau of Hong Kong, totaling approximately HK$8 million. He has published more than 150 articles in SSCI-indexed journals, as well as book chapters, edited volumes, and monographs. He has also served as an editor for several international journals. His research covers music education, creative teaching, cultural identity, arts technology, and the transmission of intangible cultural heritage. According to Stanford University's ranking of the world's top scientists, he is listed among the top 2% of Scientists in Music for 2024-2026, underscoring his outstanding international academic standing. Professor LEUNG has supervised approximately 30 doctoral graduates researching topics such as piano education, cultural identity, the integration of technology and intangible cultural heritage, parental involvement, cultural tourism, and traditional arts. He currently serves as a consultant to multiple important academic and social institutions, continuing to make significant contributions to arts education and cultural transmission. Professor LEUNG earned a Bachelor of Arts (Music) and a Diploma in Education from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, a Master of Arts from Hong Kong Baptist University, and a Doctor of Philosophy from the University of New South Wales in Australia.
