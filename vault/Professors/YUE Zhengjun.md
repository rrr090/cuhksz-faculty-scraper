## Biography

Dr. YUE Zhengjun is an Assistant Professor at the Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI), an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and a Visiting Researcher at King’s College London. Dr. Yue received a B.Eng. in Telecommunications Engineering from Shanghai University (2017), an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Edinburgh (2018), and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Sheffield (2022), supported by the EU Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions. After doctoral study, Dr. Yue conducted postdoctoral research at King’s College London and served as a tenured Assistant Professor at Delft University of Technology. Research interests include inclusive, trustworthy and explainable speech AI for healthcare, pathological and atypical speech, speech foundation models, cognitive-health monitoring, multimodal digital biomarkers, and assistive conversational agents. The work has appeared in IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, Computer Speech & Language, ICASSP, and Interspeech. Dr. Yue co-authored a paper receiving the Speech Pathology Best Paper Award at Interspeech 2026.
