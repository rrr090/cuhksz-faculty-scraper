## Biography

Dr. Gao is currently an Associate Professor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his bachelor’s degree from Wuhan University, followed by an M.Phil. in Theoretical Physics and a Ph.D. in Operations Research from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Between his master’s and doctoral studies, he worked in industry for two years. Dr. Gao’s research focuses on platform operations management, with particular interests in modeling complex customer behavior, improving the theoretical foundations of industry-standard algorithms, and designing platform regulation mechanisms. His work has been published in leading journals such as Management Science, Operations Research, and Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, as well as in top-tier CS conferences including WINE (CCF-A). He has received several research awards, including First Prize at ISCOM and Second Prize at POMS-China, and his work has been featured in major media outlets such as Hong Kong Economic Times and FT Chinese. His research has been supported by NFSC, Meituan, Didi, and Feng1.com. Several of his Ph.D. students have taken faculty positions at leading universities, including the University of New South Wales, Tongji University, and Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.
