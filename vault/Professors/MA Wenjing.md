## Biography

MA Wenjing is currently an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. She received her B.S. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2014 and 2017, respectively. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Informatics from Emory University in 2023 and completed her postdoctoral training in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Michigan. Dr. Ma’s research focuses on developing computational and statistical methods for the accurate and efficient analysis of genomics data, including spatial, single-cell, and multi-omics. Methodologically, she is interested in integrating statistical modeling with computational frameworks to incorporate biological priors. Biologically, her work aims to understand cellular dynamics during development and disease progression by leveraging temporal information across multiple omics modalities. She also collaboratively works on projects that apply her methodological research to advance disease mechanism understanding and treatment. Her work has been published in high-impact journals such as Nature Communications, Genome Biology, and Briefings in Bioinformatics. She received the Student Paper Award from the American Statistical Association in 2023 and the 1st Place Young Scientist Excellence Award from MCBIOS 2023. She was also honored with the Women in Natural Sciences Fellowship from Emory University.
