## Biography

WANG Lixu is currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Northwestern University in 2024 and his B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2020. From 2025 to 2026, he conducted postdoctoral research at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. His research focuses on data governance, including domain generalization, federated learning, machine unlearning, differential privacy, and secure multiparty computation. He has published or had nearly 30 papers accepted at leading international conferences and journals in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer security, and databases, including ICLR, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, AAAI, IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, ACM CCS, SIGMOD, and VLDB, including multiple oral presentations. He was a recipient of the 2023-2024 IBM Ph.D. Fellowship, awarded to only ten recipients worldwide. As a co-investigator, he has participated in the Singapore Ministry of Education Tier 1 project, and as the student participant, he has contributed for multiple US NSF projects. He has also conducted research internships and visits at Sony in Japan and General Motors in the United States.
