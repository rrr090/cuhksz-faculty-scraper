## Biography

Adjunct Assistant Professor LIANG graduated from Shanxi Medical University in 2010, and obtained her master's degree of surgery from Guangdong Medical University in 2013. Since 2015, she have been working in the Thyroid and Breast Surgery Department of Longgang District People's Hospital of Shenzhen. In clinical practice, Adjunct Assistant Professor LIANG is proficient in various surgical procedures for thyroid and breast diseases (especially minimally invasive cosmetic surgical procedures), early diagnosis techniques for thyroid and breast tumors (such as:US-FNA of thyroid nodules and US-CNB of breast nodules), the precise and comprehensive treatment of breast cancer. Her main research areas include: the clinical efficacy and principle of traditional Chinese medicine in the treatment of non-puerperal mastitis, the clinical efficacy and principle of acupuncture on Hashimoto’s thyroiditis.
