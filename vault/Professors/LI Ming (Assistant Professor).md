## Biography

Dr. LI Ming is an Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He is currently leading projects funded by the NSFC and the Guangdong Basic and Applied Basic Research Foundation. He maintains close collaborations with National University of Singapore, Nanyang Technological University, Carnegie Mellon University, ByteDance Seed, and Meta. Dr. LI has extensive experience in student mentorship. He has guided numerous undergraduate, master’s, and Ph.D. interns from scratch to publishing their first top-tier conference papers in computer vision and machine learning. Many of his mentees have subsequently received offers from leading technology giants in China or been admitted to fully funded Ph.D. programs at top universities in the United States. Dr. LI has published over 50 papers in leading international journals and conferences. Among them, more than 25 papers were published as first author or corresponding author. In addition, Dr. Li serves as an Associate Editor for the top-tier journals IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics and Pattern Recognition, and as an Area Chair for WACV 2026. He also received the ICML 2026 Silver Reviewer Award. His research has gained broad recognition from leading experts in the field as well as mainstream media outlets, world-class universities, top industrial research labs, and industry partners such as Huawei and Gaorong Ventures. Several of his works have been recognized as ESI Highly Cited Papers and selected for Oral presentations at premier international conferences.
