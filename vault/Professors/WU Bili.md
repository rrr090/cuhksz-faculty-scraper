## Biography

Lecturer WU has achieved balanced development in the fields of medicine, teaching and research. In terms of medical treatment, Lecturer Wu Bili has more than 10 years of working experience in obstetrics and gynecology, focusing on high-risk pregnancy management, postpartum pelvic floor rehabilitation diagnosis and treatment, and midwifery technology; Participated in the compilation of the professional book "Obstetrics and Gynecology Diagnosis and Treatment Collection". Teaching: Lecturer WU served as a clinical teacher of the School of Medicine of the Chinese University of Hong Kong, a clinical teacher of Hubei University of Medicine, and a clinical teacher of the Second Affiliated Hospital of the Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen); With teacher certificates: standardized training teachers for residents in general practice (national level), planning training teachers for residents in Guangdong Province, backbone teachers for general practice planning training for residents in Guangdong Province. She has won the Silver Award of PBL Medical Education Case Competition of Elsevier Company, the second prize of the 10th Excellent Teaching Award of Hubei University of Medicine, the first prize of the 2024 Lecture Competition of the Second Affiliated Hospital of the Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen) and the Outstanding Teacher Award. In terms of scientific research, Lecturer WU has presided over three scientific research projects at the municipal and district levels, published more than ten professional papers in domestic journals as the first author, has two utility model patents, and served as a reviewer of "China Medical Science" and "Maternal and Child Health Guide" magazines.
