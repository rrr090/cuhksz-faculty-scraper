## Biography

Professor Brian Tomlinson is a Professor at School of Medicine CUHK-Shenzhen with extensive experience in medical education in the Greater Bay Area. From 2019 to 2026 he helped establish the first medical school in Macau during his time as Professor in the Faculty of Medicine at Macau University of Science and Technology (MUST). He previously served as Professor of Medicine and Therapeutics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) from 2007 to 2015, and was Head of the Division of Clinical Pharmacology at CUHK from 2003. He also held the position of Honorary Consultant Physician at the Prince of Wales Hospital, Hong Kong, from 1990 to 2019. Professor Tomlinson has published extensively in leading journals such as The New England Journal of Medicine, The Lancet, and Nature, with an H-index of 84 and over 620 publications listed in the Web of Science Collection. He also holds research expertise and an interest in pharmacology regarding TCM compounds and drug-to-drug interactions. He collaborates with international research groups on lipid disorders and the genetics of metabolic disease, and currently serves as Chair of the Asia-Pacific Federation of the International Atherosclerosis Society (IAS). A frequent invited speaker at international conferences, he has been awarded numerous research grants and has served as an investigator in a wide range of clinical trials, including Principal Investigator in Phase 1 studies and Co-Investigator in international Phase II and III trials in hyperlipidaemia, hypertension, and diabetes.
