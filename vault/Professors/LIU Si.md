## Biography

Dr. Si Liu is an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, he was an Assistant Professor at Texas A&M University and a Senior Researcher at ETH Zurich, where he received the Career Seed Award. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. His research lies at the intersection of formal methods, databases, and distributed systems. He develops rigorous theories and techniques for building trustworthy systems, including AI-enabled systems, with strong guarantees of correctness, security, and performance. He is committed to mentoring the next generation of researchers. Dr. Si Liu is looking for motivated PhD and MPhil students to join his research group in Spring and Fall 2027. Interested candidates are encouraged to contact him to apply.
