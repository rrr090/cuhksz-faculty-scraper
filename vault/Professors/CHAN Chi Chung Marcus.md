## Biography

Prof. Marcus CHAN is a Professor of Practice in Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where he serves as the Associate Director of the Academia-Industry Collaboration Center and the Associate Director of the MPhil in AI Programme. Prof. CHAN brings extensive global experience in technology leadership, software engineering, and AI-driven productization across multinational enterprises, high-growth startup environments, and venture building. With a deep background as a technology entrepreneur and startup CTO, his career reflects a proven track record of bridging international innovation and advancing applied AI research to move complex software systems from concept to scaled commercial implementation. At CUHK-Shenzhen, Prof. CHAN is a founder and serves as the Coordinator of the Industry-University Innovation Network research group ( https://sai.cuhk.edu.cn/en/page/212 ). He designs practice-oriented AI curricula, spearheads strategic industry-university partnerships, and leads applied AI research initiatives, corporate collaborations, and incubation projects that challenge students to solve real-world industrial and entrepreneurial problems. His mentorship across both graduate and undergraduate programmes, as well as student cohorts and clubs, emphasizes global product thinking, rigorous software engineering standards, tech entrepreneurship, and the responsible deployment of AI.
