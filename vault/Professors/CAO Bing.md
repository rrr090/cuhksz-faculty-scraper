## Biography

Prof. Bing Cao specializes in synaptic plasticity and pain research. During his postdoctoral training at Stanford University, he was the first to demonstrate the role of homeostatic plasticity in the spinal dorsal horn in neuropathic pain caused by peripheral nerve injury. In 2025, he joined the School of Medicine and the Kobilka Institute of Innovative Drug Discovery at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, as an Assistant Professor. His research primarily focuses on developing small-molecular analgesics and non-invasive stimulation techniques to alleviate pain and improve cognitive function.
