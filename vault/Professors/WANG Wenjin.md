## Biography

Dr. Wenjin Wang is a distinguished young investigator in the field of chemical biology, recognized as a Young Top-notch Talent by the Guangdong Special Support Program. His research sits at the compelling intersection of chemistry and biology, focusing on the innovative use of chemical molecules to modulate critical biological processes for disease therapy. Dr. Wang’s work explores two principal areas: the regulation of biomolecular phase separation and the manipulation of intracellular aggregation. He investigates how proteins and nucleic acids organize within cells through phase separation and how this process can be controlled with metal complexes. Concurrently, he designs novel therapeutics based on aggregation-induced emission (AIE) molecules, pioneering strategies to selectively trigger their aggregation inside cells for targeted disease treatment. Since earning his Ph.D. in Chemical Biology from Sun Yat-Sen University in 2022, where he studied the antitumor mechanisms of metal complexes, Dr. Wang has continued his research at institutions affiliated with The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Currently an Associate Research Fellow at the Second Affiliated Hospital and Adjunct Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. His prolific research has resulted in numerous high-impact publications in leading journals such as Nature Photonics , Journal of the American Chemical Society , Angewandte Chemie , and Nature Communications , including multiple highly cited papers. As a Principal Investigator, Dr. Wang has secured competitive funding from the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Shenzhen Natural Science Foundation and the Shenzhen Medical Research Fund. His contributions to the field have been recognized with several prestigious awards, including the National Scholarship for Doctoral Students, the Excellent Postdoctoral Fellows of Shenzhen and the Young Scientist Award at a major international oncology conference.
