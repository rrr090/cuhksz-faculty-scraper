## Biography

Adjunct Assistant Professor ZHANG Graduated from Shandong University in 2006 for bachelor degree, and graduated from Shantou University for master degree. He was engaged in orthopedic microsurgery for the first decade. From 2017, he began to specialize in sports medicine and bone joint, and deeply research the diagnosis and treatment of shoulder, elbow, knee, knee and ankle sports injuries and minimally invasive arthroscopic treatment ; stepped treatment for hip and knee osteoarthritis . Adjunct Assistant Professor ZHANG now is the Member of the second Committee of Sports Medicine Committee of Shenzhen Association of Traditional Chinese Medicine since 2023 to 2027, Member of The first Committee of the Sports Medicine Professional Committee of the Guangdong Clinical Medical Association since 2021 to 2026, Member of the First Joint Surgery Professional Committee of Guangdong Clinical Medical Society since 2020 to 2025, Member of the second Joint Surgery Branch of Shenzhen Medical Association since 2024 to 2028, Member of Trauma and Orthopedics Professional Committee of Shenzhen Hospital Association since 2022, Member of the first orthopedic Minimally Invasive Professional Committee of Shenzhen Society of Traditional Chinese Medicine since 2023, Member of the Clinical Skills Training and Guidance Professional Committee of Guangdong Grassroots Medical Association since 2021.
