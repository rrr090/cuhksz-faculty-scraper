## Biography

Professor David ZHANG is X.Q. Deng Presidential Chair Professor in the Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), Director of GuangDong Engineering Technology Research Center of Generalized Human body Information Perception and Pattern Analysis, Director of Shenzhen Key Laboratory of Pattern Analysis and Perceptual Computing, Emeritus Chair Professor of Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Prof. David ZHANG graduated in Computer Science from Peking University. He received his MSc in 1982 and his PhD in 1985 in both Computer Science from the Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), respectively. He was the first PhD in AI area in People’s Republic of China. From 1986 to 1988 he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Tsinghua University and then an Associate Professor at the Academia Sinica, Beijing. In 1994 he received his second PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. He has been a Chair Professor at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University where he is the Founding Director of Biometrics Research Centre (UGC/CRC) supported by the Hong Kong SAR Government since 2005. Currently he is Presidential Chair Professor in Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen). He also serves as Visiting Chair Professor in Tsinghua University and HIT, and Adjunct Professor in Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Peking University, National University of Defense Technology and the University of Waterloo. He is both Founder and Editor-in-Chief of International Journal of Image & Graphics (IJIG) and Springer International Series on Biometrics (KISB); Organizer of the first International Conference on Biometrics Authentication (ICBA); and Associate Editor of more than ten international journals including IEEE Transactions and so on. Over past 40 years, he has been working on pattern recognition, image processing and biometrics, where many research results have been awarded and some created directions, including palmprint recognition, computerized TCM and facial beauty analysis, are famous in the world. So far, he has published over 30 monographs, 600 international journal papers and 70 patents from USA/Japan/China. In 2025, he awarded the "Wu Wenjun Artificial Intelligence Technology Contribution Award". He has been continuously listed as a Highly Cited Researchers for many years. From 2020 to 2026, he has been continuously listed as World's Top 2% Scientists. From 2022 to 2025, he has been continuously listed as China Leader Award in Computer Science. He is ranked 44 in the world (ranked 5 in China) with H-Index 144 at Research.com Best Computer Science Scientists Ranking in 2026 (https://research.com/scientists-rankings/computer-science). He is ranked 82 in the world (ranked 3 in China) in Computer Science at ScholarGPS Top 0.05% of 30 million scholars worldwide in 2026. Respectively for his outstanding achievements in the field of biometrics, Prof. ZHANG was selected as a Fellow of both the Royal Society of Canada and the Canadian Academy of Engineering in 2020 and 2021. Professor Zhang is also an IAPR Fellow, an AAIA Fellow, AIIA (International Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance) Fellow and an IEEE Life Fellow.
