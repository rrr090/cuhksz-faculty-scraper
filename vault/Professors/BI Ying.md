## Biography

Adjunct Assistant professor (clinical) Ying Bi from the Baotou Medical College in 2008 and obtained her master degree from the Sun Yat-sen University in 2017. She is a board-certified anesthesiologist with 15 years of experience in the field. She worked as Associate chief physician in Department of Anesthesia, 2nd Affiliated Hospital of CUHK(SZ) Longgang District People’s Hospital. Throughout her career, she worked in various clinical settings, including Pain clinic and operating theatre, gaining extensive experience in providing anesthesia for a wide range of surgical procedures. Specializations: difficult airway, neonatal or aged patients’ anesthesia and ultrasound guided pain treatment as well as regional anesthesia. Her research area related to ultrasound guided nerve block and neurotic effect of anesthesia on developing brain. Adjunct Assistant professor (clinical) Bi is the Youth committee member of the pain of Shenzhen Medical Association.
