## Biography

Professor Yunyi Zhang is an Assistant Professor of School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before then he was a Ph.D. student at the University of California San Diego. He has published papers in several top statistics journals, including Annals of Statistics, Information and Inference: A Journal of the IMA, and Electronic Journal of Statistics. He was a reviewer for Journal of Time Series Analysis.
