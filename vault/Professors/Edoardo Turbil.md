## Biography

Edoardo Turbil currently serves as an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. In 2025, he received CUHK(SZ)’s Presidential Exemplary Teaching Award (PETA) and the School of Music Young Researcher Award (MUS YR), recognizing the excellence and impact of his teaching, artistic activity, and research. He is regarded as one of the most brilliant Italian pianists of his generation. Praised by leading figures in the international music community, including Paul Badura-Skoda and Solomon Mikowsky, he has been described as an “excellent pianist, with sensitivity and a well-developed technique,” and as an artist “able to satisfy the highest artistic expectations.” “Edoardo Turbil mounts the wild beast [Prokofiev’s Piano Sonata no. 8] and rides it to the end. There’s never any question as to who’s in charge. His technical mastery is not just impressive, it’s riveting.” — Fanfare , May/June 2023 “Ye Xiaogang’s Falling Flowers is filled with poetic elegance, with delicate and varied harmonies and colors, reminiscent of impressionistic music, while incorporating the temperament and charm of Cantonese music. Edoardo Turbil’s performance is refined and subtle, capturing the essence perfectly, displaying a unique understanding and interpretation of Chinese musical style and temperament.” — Music Weekly , Beijing, June 2023 As an acclaimed Scarlatti specialist, Turbil has performed as a recitalist, concerto soloist, and chamber musician in prestigious concert halls and festivals worldwide. His appearances include Shenzhen Concert Hall, Spoleto Festival of the Two Worlds, Frankfurt Alte Oper Mozart Saal, Turin Conservatory Theater “G. Verdi,” New York Alice Tully Hall, Bologna Accademia Filarmonica Sala Mozart, Zurich Zunfthaus zur Waag, Hong Kong Lee Hysan Concert Hall, Beijing Central Conservatory Recital Hall, Teatro Regio in Turin, and other venues across Europe, America, and Asia. As a concerto soloist, he has appeared with orchestras including the Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra, New Juilliard Ensemble, Orchestra Filarmonica del ’900, and Galilei Orchestra. Turbil’s distinctive and eclectic artistry reflects a synthesis of the great Italian and American piano traditions. His repertoire ranges from the classics to lesser-performed composers such as Clementi, Sinigaglia, Villa-Lobos, Debussy, Messiaen, Granados, Albéniz, de Falla, Stockhausen, Soler, and Say. His Scarlatti expertise was shaped by his formative studies with Maria Tipo, one of the world’s leading Scarlatti interpreters. He received the Best Performance of a Baroque Work Award at the Chicago International Music Competition for his interpretations of Scarlatti sonatas, and in 2025 released Domenico Scarlatti: 20 Sonatas with NCPA Classics. In 2021, Turbil earned a Doctor of Musical Arts degree from The Juilliard School’s C.V. Starr Doctoral Piano Performance Program under the mentorship of Dr. Yoheved Kaplinsky. His dissertation, “Leone Sinigaglia: A Rediscovery of Brahms’s Italian Apostle,” received Juilliard’s Richard F. French Doctoral Prize. He previously studied with Maria Tipo, Solomon Mikowsky, and Andrea Lucchesini. Throughout his career, Turbil has received numerous awards and distinctions, including the Gold Medal with High Distinction at the Manhattan International Music Competition, Third Grand Prize and Best Performance of a Baroque Work Award at the Chicago International Music Competition, First Prize at the 28th World Music Competition, a nomination for the German Piano Award, the Melvin Jones Fellowship from Lions Club International, and recognition through the City-level Talents of Shenzhen Program. His solo recordings are featured on NCPA Classics, Da Vinci Records, Centaur Records, and KNS Classical. In addition to his performance career, Turbil is a dedicated educator. He has taught at The Juilliard School, the Turin Conservatory, and the Livorno Conservatory, and has served as a substitute teacher for Pre-College Piano Major at The Tianjin Juilliard School. He is frequently invited to teach masterclasses at institutions including Shenzhen Lang Lang Music World, Shanghai Polar Music Center, Shenzhen Arts School, Associazione Musicale Rospigliosi, and Dongguan EGW Musician Art Center. He regularly serves as a jury member for competitions such as the Macao Piano Competition Finals, Shenzhen Lang Lang Music World Piano Concerto Competition, Ningbo Piano Competition, Zhuhai Piano Competition, Xiamen International Piano Competition, Guangzhou Blüthner International Festival, and other international and regional competitions. Turbil has held leadership positions with the World Piano Teachers Association, serving as Piano Pedagogy Chapter President from 2023 to 2026. He has also served as Artistic Director of the Centro Pecci Piano Festival in Prato, Italy.
