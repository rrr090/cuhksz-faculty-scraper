## Biography

Dr. Sun Rui joined School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen as an assistant professor (teaching) in 2023. She was a visiting scholar at School of Public Health in the University of Toronto from April to October 2016. In 2017, she obtained her Ph.D. in biostatistics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and subsequently worked as a postdoctoral fellow at the School of Public Health and Primary Care of The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Starting in 2020, she became an associate researcher and associate director of the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at The Seventh Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University. Her main research interests include the development of biostatistical and bioinformatics methods, and the analysis of gene-gene and gene-environment interactions in complex diseases. Dr. Sun Rui has published more than 20 papers in international journals and has received awards such as the Research Talent Development Award of Hong Kong SAR.
