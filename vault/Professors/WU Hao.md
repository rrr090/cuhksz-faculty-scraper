## Biography

Dr. Hao Wu will join the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen as an Assistant Professor in January 2027. He is currently a William T. Tutte Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Waterloo, hosted by Professor Florian Kerschbaum. Before joining Waterloo, he was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Copenhagen, where he was affiliated with Basic Algorithms Research Copenhagen (BARC) and hosted by Professor Rasmus Pagh. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Melbourne under the supervision of Professor Anthony Wirth and Professor Olga Ohrimenko. His research focuses on scalable privacy-preserving data analytics and learning-augmented algorithms. He develops efficient algorithms for differential privacy, data sketching, graph analytics, and privacy-preserving distributed systems, with an emphasis on combining practical performance with rigorous mathematical guarantees. His work has been published at leading conferences, including PODS, ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, SIGMOD, AISTATS, and KDD. His papers were selected for a spotlight presentation at ICLR 2025 and an oral presentation at ICML 2023. He received the William T. Tutte Postdoctoral Fellowship in 2024.
