## Biography

Stephen Ni-Hahn is currently an Assistant Professor of the School of Data Science, CUHK-Shenzhen. Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Associate at Duke University, where he also earned his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering. His research investigates generative AI for computational creativity, specifically incorporating domain knowledge to enable greater interpretability and controllability in AI music systems. His work has been featured and published broadly in many top machine learning and music conferences such as NeurIPS, AAAI, KDD, ISMIR, and SMT.
