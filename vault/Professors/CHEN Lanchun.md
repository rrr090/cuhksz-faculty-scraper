## Biography

Doctor Lanchun Chen graduated from the University of Jinan with master’s degree in 2009. She started her career in Longgang district people’s hospital of Shenzhen in 2009. She completed her fellowship of intensive care medicine in Prince of Wales hospital in Hongkong in 2020. She obtained associate consultant certificate of intensive care medicine in 2021 and was appointed as the Deputy Director of the ICU department in 2022. Dr Lanchun Chen was employed to be the course instructor of BASIC (Basic Assessment & Support of Critical Ill patients) course in 2023. She is the member of the Critical Care Medicine Committee of the Medical Association of Shenzhen from 2022 to 2026. Her research area related to sepsis, ECMO, blood purification and ventilation in brain injury. She has published several articles on these areas.
