## Biography

Professor Lin is a psychiatrist and researcher with nearly two decades of clinical and academic expertise in mental health, with a specialization in mood disorders. He holds a PhD in Psychology from the University of Hong Kong. His research program integrates clinical translation, digital phenotyping, and health behavior science to advance early detection, identify predictive biomarkers, and develop non-pharmacological interventions for depression and bipolar disorder, with a focus on high-risk populations. His work employs innovative methodologies, including computational psychiatry and randomized controlled trials, to evaluate novel treatment strategies such as transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS), Lycium barbarum polysaccharide, and structured exercise programs. Through this translational approach, Professor Lin aims to establish accessible, evidence-based interventions that expand the therapeutic landscape beyond conventional pharmacotherapy and promote precision mental health. Professor Lin has published over 100 peer-reviewed articles in journals, including Nature Mental Health, American Journal of Psychiatry, Biological Psychiatry, JAMA Psychiatry , and Clinical Psychology Review . His work integrates cognitive assessments, neuroimaging, biomarkers, and behavioural measures to develop models that detect mood disorders before clinical onset, supporting earlier intervention and personalised care. Professor Lin serves as Vice President of the Guangdong Brain Science Application Association and is a Committee Member of the Bipolar Disorder Working Committee of the Chinese Medical Association’s Society of Psychiatry. He also contributed to the ISBD Task Force Consensus Study. His research has been supported by the Hong Kong Research Grants Council and the National Natural Science Foundation of China.
