## Biography

Jiang Tiefeng is a tenured professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Minnesota and a recipient of the NSF Career Award. He is mainly engaged in research in probability, statistics and related fields, especially in interdisciplinary subjects such as probability theory, high-dimensional statistics and pure mathematics. He has made breakthrough progress. The result of Professor Jiang's solution of "Haar unitary matrix is approximated by independent random variables" has been used in the research of quantum computing. Professor Jiang has published more than 50 papers, most of which have been published in top international probability, statistics and machine learning magazines, including "Ann. Probab.", "Probab. Theor. Rel. Fields", "Ann. Stat." ", "Ann. Appl. Probab.", "J. Mach. Learn Res.", etc.
