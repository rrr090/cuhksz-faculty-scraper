## Biography

Prof. Juan DU received her Bachelor Degree of Clinical Medicine from School of Medicine at Jiamusi University, Master Degree of Physiology from Anhui Medical University, and her Ph.D. Degree from the School of Biomedical Sciences at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. From 2004 to 2020, Prof. Du served as Professor of Physiology, Vice Dean of the School of Basic Medical Sciences, and Director of the National Simulation Experimental Center for Basic Medicine at Anhui Medical University, with a term as Visiting Scholar at the University of North Texas Health Science Center from 2005 to 2006. In 2020, Prof. Du joined The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen and contributed to the preparation of School of Medicine. Currently, she is a tenured Associate Professor and Assistant Dean in School of Medicine at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Prof. Du’s research mainly focus on calcium signaling and cellular function, tumor exosome secretion mechanisms, early cancer diagnostics, and exosome-based targeted delivery. She has led four NSFC grants and 20 other projects, and published 80+ SCI papers in JASN, Theranostics, ACS Nano, iMeta , etc. Prof. Du is committed to advancing medical education, driving pedagogical reforms, and fostering students’ innovative capabilities. She has spearheaded 10 educational initiatives, including a Ministry of Education Industry-University Collaboration Program and provincial-level MOOC demonstration projects, earning her the title of “Provincial Master Teacher.” As editor or co-editor of 10 textbooks. She has mentored students to over 20 national and provincial accolades.
