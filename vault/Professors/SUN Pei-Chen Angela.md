## Biography

Prof. Sun graduated in Medicine from the University of Aberdeen and completed her paediatric training in the UK. During higher specialist training, she was awarded a Chief Scientist Office (CSO) PhD Fellowship in Child Health. She has been a paediatric consultant in the UK since 2013 and is a Fellow of the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health (RCPCH). Her clinical interests include paediatric diabetes and endocrinology, and she is a member of the International Society for Pediatric and Adolescent Diabetes (ISPAD) and the European Society for Paediatric Endocrinology (ESPE). During her previous appointments as Honorary Clinical Senior Lecturer at the Universities of Aberdeen and Edinburgh, she was actively involved in medical education and curriculum development. She has also contributed to MRCPCH examination question development, reflecting her broader commitment to the education and assessment of paediatric trainees.Her commitment to medical education is further supported by a Postgraduate Certificate in Clinical Education and experience as a UK Advanced Paediatric Life Support (APLS) instructor. Prof. Sun believes that high-quality medical education should integrate strong clinical foundations with patient safety, reflective professional practice, and effective clinical decision-making. As a clinician-educator with experience across both UK and Chinese healthcare systems, she places particular emphasis on developing learners who are clinically competent, ethically grounded, reflective and able to adapt to diverse healthcare environments. Her educational approach emphasises clinical reasoning, patient safety, situational awareness, teamwork and communication. She has a particular interest in clinical simulation as an effective educational strategy for developing these competencies and promoting safe, collaborative clinical practice.
