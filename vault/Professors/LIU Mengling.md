## Biography

LIU Menglin is currently an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, with a courtesy appointment in School of Artificial Intelligence. She earned her Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of California, Davis, and completed her doctoral training in computational social science and text-as-data methods. Her research focuses on large language models, natural language processing, and AI-driven measurement for social science, with applications to political communication, public opinion, and governance. Her work has been published in journals such as Cities and Local Government Studies , and she collaborates with scholars and AI scientists across academia and industry.
