## Biography

HUANG Xu is currently an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the University of Auckland and his Ph.D. in Control Theory and Control Engineering from Shandong University. He also holds an M.Eng. in Computer Applications and a B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Shandong University of Technology. His research focuses on intelligent perception using millimeter-wave radar, object detection and tracking, multi-sensor fusion, wireless sensor networks, and robotic navigation. His work spans lightweight embedded sensing, robust multi-radar fusion, learning-based identification and classification, and privacy-preserving perception for indoor environments and autonomous robotic platforms. He has published in Journal of Sensors, Electronics, Sensors, IEEE conference proceedings, and leading Chinese engineering journals.
