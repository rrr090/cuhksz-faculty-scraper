## Biography

Dr. Chenhao Ma is an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) and a Presidential Young Fellow. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong. His research spans data management, data mining, and data-centric AI, focusing on LLM + Data (text-to-SQL, dataset search, vector search), graph data management and mining, and spatio-temporal data intelligence. He has published more than 60 papers in top venues such as SIGMOD, PVLDB, KDD, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR. His work was selected as one of four Best of SIGMOD 2020 papers (4/458) and received the ACM SIGMOD Research Highlight Award 2021. He leads research projects funded by NSFC, Guangdong Province, CCF-Ant, and Tencent, and serves as an Area Chair for NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, and ACL Rolling Review, a Senior PC member for AAAI, CIKM, and PAKDD, and a PC member for SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, and WWW.
