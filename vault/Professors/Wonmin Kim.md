## Biography

Wonmin Kim serves as an Assistant Professor (Teaching) at the School of Music, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where she is engaged in the artistic training and mentorship of young pianists. Alongside her academic work, she maintains an active international career as a concert pianist and chamber musician, performing across North and South America, Europe, and Asia, and is recognized for her versatility as both a soloist and collaborative artist. As a soloist, she has appeared with orchestras such as the Bern Symphony Orchestra, the Seoul Symphony Orchestra, and the Busan Symphony Orchestra, presenting a wide range of concertos by Chopin, Saint-Saëns, Beethoven, and Mozart. Her performances have been noted for their technical command and interpretive depth, including a recent appearance in Beethoven's Triple Concerto with the Busan Symphony Orchestra, in which she was praised for her "spectacular technique and overwhelming immersion." A committed chamber musician, Kim is a member of the Shanghai New Music Ensemble and co-founder of the LAETUS Duo with violinist Juyoung Kim. She has performed and collaborated with artists such as Ilya Gringolts, Paul Huang, Chee-Yun, Wolfgang Schmidt, Stefan Milenkovich, André Cazalet, and the late Igor Ozim, Robert Mann, as well as with members of leading ensembles including the Metropolitan Opera Orchestra, the New York Philharmonic, the Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra, the Shanghai Symphony Orchestra, and the Suzhou Symphony Orchestra. Her engagements have brought her to major international venues including Carnegie Hall (Weill Recital Hall), Lincoln Center (Alice Tully Hall), the Kimmel Center, Strathmore, Tonhalle Zürich, Kioi Hall in Tokyo, Seoul Arts Center, Sejong Center for the Performing Arts, and the Shanghai Oriental Art Center. She has also been featured in the World Masters Series at Kumho Art Hall in Seoul with violinist Igor Ozim, a performance broadcast by KBS and Arirang TV. Her media presence includes broadcasts on WQXR, Swiss Radio International, and KBS, as well as the recording Korean Musician for the 21st Century released under KBS. Previously, she held teaching and collaborative piano positions at institutions including The Juilliard School, Soochow University, DePauw University, and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. At Juilliard, she worked closely with artists and faculty such as the Juilliard String Quartet, Itzhak Perlman, Hyo Kang, Donald Weilerstein, and the late Dorothy DeLay. Her festival affiliations include the Aspen Music Festival and School, the Mozarteum Summer Academy in Salzburg, the Meadowmount School of Music, the Great Mountain International Music Festival in South Korea, the Green Mountain Chamber Music Festival, and the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music Summer String Academy. Kim received The Juilliard School’s Vladimir Horowitz Award with a full-tuition scholarship and holds degrees from the Hochschule der Künste Bern and The Juilliard School. Her artistic development has been shaped by mentorship from Jungjoo Oh, Kyung Sook Lee, Michael Studer, Jonathan Feldman, Margo Garrett, and Samuel Sanders.
