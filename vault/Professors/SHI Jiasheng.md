## Biography

Jiasheng Shi is an Assistant Professor of School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Prior joining CUHK-Shenzhen in 2023, he was a Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and a Postdoc at the Department of Biostatistics, University of Pennsylvania, and the Policy Lab, Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia. He has published papers in several top statistics and public health journals, including Annals of Statistics, Annals of Applied Statistics, Health Affairs, and Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association. He has served as a reviewer for multiple journals and actively participated in academic serving works.
