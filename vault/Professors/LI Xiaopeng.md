## Biography

Dr. LI Xiaopeng is an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research from Columbia University in 2025. Prior to that, he earned his B.S. in Applied Mathematics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2020. Dr. LI’s research focuses on first-order methods for solving nonconvex and nonsmooth optimization problems, with applications in data science areas such as matrix sensing, neural networks, and reasoning in large language models (LLMs). His work lies at the intersection of variational analysis, dynamical systems, and real algebraic geometry. His research has been published in top journals including SIAM Journal on Optimization (SIOPT) .
