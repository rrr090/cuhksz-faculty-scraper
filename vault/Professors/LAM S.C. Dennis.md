## Biography

Professor Dennis Lam is an accomplished clinician-scientist and a global leader in ophthalmology. He currently serves as the Director of the Primasia International Eye Research Institute (PIERI), The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen and is a Life Fellow of the Academia Ophthalmologica Internationalis (AOI) (limited to 100 members worldwide). With over 40 years in medicine, he focuses on complex eye diseases and major blinding conditions. He has published over 900 SCI-indexed papers with an H-index of 90, including publications in The Lancet, New England Journal of Medicine, and Nature Reviews Disease Primers. Beyond incorporating his research into international diagnostic/treatment guidelines, he pioneered Consensus Statement standards, which were highly acclaimed and adopted by 7 Asia-Pacific ophthalmic specialty societies for developing new guidelines. He was the sole Chinese ophthalmologist elected as a "World Top 0.05% Scholar" by ScholarGPS (2024) and is the sole recipient of the AAPPO Innovator Award (2024). He has been on the "Top 100 Ophthalmologists in the World" Power List for seven consecutive years (2024: #1 in Asia, #6 globally). He founded the Asia-Pacific Journal of Ophthalmology (APJO) in 2012, which now ranks #1 in Asia-Pacific and #4 globally (JCR 2026). He has served as President of the Asia-Pacific Academy of Ophthalmology (APAO) and founded 8 APAC subspecialty societies and 3 international organizations (Academia Retina Internationalis , ARI; Global Ophthalmic AI and Technology Society , GOATS; International Society of Oncology Prevention and Treatments , ISOPT ). Professor Lam is also dedicated to public service and talent development. He has served as a Deputy of the National People's Congress (NPC) since 2008, and a Legislative Councilor of Hong Kong (2022-2025). He founded "Project Vision" and advocated for China's "Million Cataract Surgeries Program". For decades, he has mentored hundreds of ophthalmologists and postgraduate students, with seven of whom being named "Ten Outstanding Young Persons (TOYPA) of Hong Kong". He facilitated China's entry into APAO (2006) and secured APAO Congresses in Mainland China (2010, 2015). He also organized the 2008 World Ophthalmology Congress, significantly advancing China's integration into international ophthalmology. His contributions earned him the World Outstanding Chinese Award. In recognition of his work, APAO and APVRS established the annual "Dennis Lam Lecture" awards. In 2025, he ranked #1 on the "Asia-Pacific Power List 100" jointly organized by AAPPO, APAO, and APJO. WEBSITE OF PIERI https://www.pieri.ac.cn/
