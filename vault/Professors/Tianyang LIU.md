## Biography

Tianyang Liu is Associate Professor in the School of Public Policy at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his PhD from the University of Melbourne, Australia, and subsequently served for six years as a tenured Associate Professor at Wuhan University. His research lies at the intersection of international security, political sociology, and political communication, with a particular focus on Chinese politics and society, non-Western political thought, and comparative security studies. His scholarship has appeared in international journals , including International Affairs, Political Geography, Critical Asian Studies, The Pacific Review, Terrorism and Political Violence, Contemporary Politics, International Relations of the Asia-Pacific, Asian Studies Review, Political Studies Review, and International Communication Gazette, among others. He is the author of China’s Soft War on Terror: Space-Making Processes of Securitisation (Routledge, 2022) and The Chinese Geography of Ordinary Security (Palgrave Macmillan, 2025), and co-author of Chinese Paradiplomacy at the Peripheries: Beyond the Hinterland (Routledge, 2024), alongside several other books on Chinese politics, society, and statecraft. He has also written extensively in non-English academic works.
