## Biography

Lecturer WU shan graduated from Shantou University Medical College in 2011, major in Neurology, then she worked in the Department of Neurology, The Second Affiliated Hospital, CUHK-Shenzhen. Lecturer WU had engaged in clinical medical practice and teaching in the field of neurology for a long period, specialized in the diagnosis and treatment of cerebrovascular diseases, dizziness, headache, epilepsy. She had rich experience in early diagnosis and treatment of acute ischemic stroke, also in the quality control of stroke green channel. Now Lecturer WU is committee member of the Shenzhen Brain Neurophysiology Association, and committee member of the Neurocritical Care Branch of Shenzhen Stroke Society.
