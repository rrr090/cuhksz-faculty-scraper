## Biography

Prof. Yujie Ren is an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before joining CUHK Shenzhen, he received his Ph.D. degree from Rutgers University in 2023 and has been a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL since 2024. He builds data infrastructures, with a focus on operating system designs for heterogeneous hardware (storage and memory) and operating system support for semantic-rich applications such as LLM and AI agents. His work has appeared in top tier system and AI conferences including OSDI, SOSP, FAST, ASPLOS, USENIX ATC, ICLR, as well as journals such as ACM Transactions on Storage. Prof. Ren’s research is closely connected to industry, including a research internship at NVIDIA on OS support for GPU storage access and several world-wide patents resulting from collaborations with Samsung on storage subsystems. His recent work on an OS blueprint for AI agents has been widely adopted by the open-source community, including the AIOS Foundation project with over 5,000 GitHub stars.
