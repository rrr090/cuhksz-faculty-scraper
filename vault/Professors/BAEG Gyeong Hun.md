## Biography

Prof. BAEG Gyeong Hun received his PhD in Biomedicine at the Osaka University Graduate School of Medicine, Japan, where he conducted cancer research. To expand knowledge of cancer biology, joined the laboratory of Prof. Norbert Perrimon at Harvard Medical School, a postdoctoral fellow at the Howard Hughes School of Medicine, and used Drosophila as an in vivo model system to study Wnt and Jak in relation to tumorigenesis and progression. Molecules were characterized by Stat et al. In 2006, he joined the Department of Pediatrics at New York Medical College as an assistant professor, focusing on the role of Jak-Stat signaling in pediatric cancers, especially acute lymphoblastic leukemia. In 2012, he moved to Singapore and worked as an assistant professor in the Department of Anatomy, National University of Singapore. Using Drosophila genetics, we investigated the role of the JAK STAT signaling pathway and redox signaling in stem cell behavior and cancer regulation, and investigated the molecular mechanisms of nanomaterial-mediated cytotoxicity and genotoxicity. Bringing together genetic, genomic, molecular and cell biology techniques enables a systems-level understanding of these complex processes. Has served as a special reviewer for scientific journals and funding agencies, including biomaterials, cell death and differentiation, and small. He has also been appointed as a member of the Academic Editor/Editorial Board of numerous international authoritative journals and book series. He is interested in teaching human anatomy, including histology to medical, pharmacy and life science students.
