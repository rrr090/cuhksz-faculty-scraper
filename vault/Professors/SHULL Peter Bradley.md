## Biography

Peter Bradley SHULL is currently a Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. He received his B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and Computer Engineering from LeTourneau University in 2005, and his M.S. and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Stanford University in 2008 and 2012, respectively. He completed his postdoctoral training in the Department of Bioengineering at Stanford University in 2013 under the supervision of David Camarillo. He joined Shanghai Jiao Tong University as an Assistant Professor in 2013, was promoted to Associate Professor in 2016, and to full Professor in 2022. His laboratory develops wearable systems to explore principles of embodied AI and perception, human movement and movement modification. His team combines robotic, haptic, biomechanics, and machine learning principles to create unique sensors, real-time models, AI and physics-based fusion algorithms, and novel feedback devices. Target applications include realistic embodied somatosensation in autonomous and teleoperated humanoids, improved medical treatment for osteoarthritis and stroke, and optimized athletic performance for golf and running. He has published over 100 peer-reviewed journal articles in leading venues including IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, Nature npj Digital Medicine, and Journal of Biomechanics, among others. He has received numerous honors including the Shanghai Jiao Tong University President's Award (Top 0.1%) in 2025, the Outstanding Researcher Award from the United States National Institutes of Health (NIH) National Center for Simulation in Rehabilitation in 2017, and the Shanghai Young Talents Award in 2014. He serves as Deputy Editor for the Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation and Associate Editor for Nature npj Digital Medicine and IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics.
