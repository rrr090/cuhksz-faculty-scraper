## Biography

Dr. WU Baoyuan is a tenured Associate Professor and Assistant Dean (Research) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). His research interests include trustworthy AI, Machine Learning, and Computer Vision. He has published over 120 papers in top-tier AI venues, including 16 papers in TPAMI and IJCV, with over 13,000 Google Scholar citations. He serves as Senior Area Editor of IEEE TIFS, and has served as Area Chair of CVPR, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, and AAAI. He has been selected to Stanford University's "World's Top 2% Scientists" list for multiple consecutive years. As Principal Investigator, he has led several major research projects funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology, the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and Guangdong Province, and has maintained long-term collaborations with Tencent, Huawei, and Kuaishou. He received the Young Researcher Award of CUHK-Shenzhen in 2023.
