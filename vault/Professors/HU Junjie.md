## Biography

Professor HU Junjie received his Ph.D. in System Information Engineering from Tohoku University, Japan, in 2020. From 2020 to 2025, he served as a Research Scientist at the Shenzhen Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society (AIRS). In August 2025, he joined the School of Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) as an Assistant Professor. He has long been engaged in cutting-edge research in the fields of artificial intelligence and robotics, with over 50 papers published in top-tier international journals and conferences such as TPAMI, TRO, CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, ICRA, and IROS. He has led and participated in numerous national and provincial-level research projects, including the Shenzhen Science and Technology Program, the Guangdong Basic and Applied Basic Research Foundation, the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation, the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and the National Key R&D Program of China. His research focuses on data-driven embodied intelligence, multi-sensor fusion, robot navigation and manipulation.
