## Biography

YIN Feng received his B.Sc. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China, and his M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees from Technische Universitaet Darmstadt, Germany. From 2014 to 2016, he was a postdoc researcher with Ericsson Research, Linkoping, Sweden. Since 2016, he has been with The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) and is currently an Associate Professor and an Assistant Dean (Education) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen. His research interests include statistical signal processing, Bayesian learning and optimization, and sensory data fusion. He has published more than 40 top-tier journal papers, 50 conferences, and 20 patents/standards. He was a recipient of the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-Financed Students Abroad in 2013 and the Marie Curie Young Fellowship from the European Union in 2014. He was the finalist for the IEEE CAMSAP best paper award in 2013 and received the best paper award of ICSINC conference in 2022. He has served as Associate Editor for the Elsevier Signal Processing Journal and currently serving as the Associate Editor for the IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing. He is an IEEE senior member and a core member of the IEEE Machine Learning and Signal Processing (MLSP) technical committee and the IEEE SPS scholarship committee.
